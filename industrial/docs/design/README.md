# J's Industrial: the design

This folder contains the design spec of J's Industrial. The entire mod is made based on these documents, with the sole
purpose of not only making every system that exists, and will exist, sane and easy to build by anyone, but also of
leaving no doubt about the things yet to be made.

Each page covers one subject. Inside a page, what the mod already does comes first; what is designed but not built yet
is in a section called **To build** at the end of the page, and the list at the bottom of this page gathers all of them
in one place. Every number in these pages is a first estimate, to be tuned in play.

For using the mod rather than building it, the player and developer guides are one folder up:
[the machines](../MACHINES.md) and [for mod and pack authors](../FOR_MOD_AUTHORS.md).

## To build

Everything below is designed and waiting to be built, in the order of the pages. Each item links to the page that
describes it.

- The catalogue as registries of J's Core: materials, forms, properties, host rocks and isotopes; the states of matter
  as separate kinds; every element and its uses; elements beyond 118, named by their discoverers.
  [Materials](materials.md)
- About 250 real minerals, with their host rock variants. [Minerals](minerals.md)
- Veins as real deposit types crossing rocks, their settings, prospecting, reservoirs of oil, gas and brine in reservoir
  rock, and rubber trees. [The world](world.md)
- The three levels of ore processing, forms with hand tools and dies, per-metal scraps, the steel chain and about 100
  alloys. [Metallurgy](metallurgy.md)
- The circuits of every tier and the machines that make them in quantity. [Tiers and circuits](tiers-and-circuits.md)
- J's Energy: voltage classes, loss by distance, ten cables and five overhead lines, fuses and the wrong class, the
  generators and power stations, storage, transformers, converters, control blocks and tools, shocks, lightning and
  stripped cables. [Energy](energy.md)
- What every machine has, fifteen upgrades, the Central Cooling Plant and the Compressed Air Plant, multiblock ports,
  formed models and the Structure Projector, and the machines of T1 to T5. [Machines](machines.md)
- A line for each state of matter, failing pipes, leaks and hazards, pipes and tanks by real volume, pumps, valves and
  boiler explosions, air separation, cryogenics and ultra-pure water. [Fluids and gases](fluids-and-gases.md)
- Real reactions with conditions and catalysts, pollution and its cleaning, the refinery, coal, wood and plant
  chemistry, heavy chemicals, polymers, rare earths and byproducts, specialties, mining explosives, and potions and dyes
  in quantity. [Chemistry](chemistry.md)
- Belts and their parts, pneumatic tubes, silos and crates. [Conveyors](conveyors.md)
- The Clean Room and its cleanliness, the silicon chain, the tools of T4 and T5 with lithography in three generations,
  re-entrant lots and the Overhead Hoist Transport, packaging, hardware for J's Computers, and other semiconductors.
  [Clean Room](clean-room.md)
- Research as discoveries, the CRC and its groups, instruments, accelerators, radioisotopes and the Calutron.
  [Science](science.md)
- The fuel cycle, ten reactor families, xenon and decay heat, accidents and containment, the waste path, the Tokamak and
  laser fusion, and protection from two kinds of radiation. [Nuclear](nuclear.md)
- Where the elements beyond 118 come from, the Deep Core Miner, exotic matter, antimatter and its reactor, the
  Singularity Reactor, the circuits and machines of T6 to T9, the Heart of the Universe, the Creation Forge, the Reality
  Projector, the Quantum Link and the Wormhole Gate. [Exotic materials](exotic-materials.md)
- The Aleph: its gate, its laws by region, singularities, biomes, beings, resources, the Builders' technology and
  dissonance. [The Aleph](aleph.md)
- Tools and armour by material, utility tools, electric and pneumatic tools, protective suits, four exo-suits and their
  modules. [Tools and armour](tools-and-armour.md)
- With J's Computers: the ICC and its Fieldbus Cards, machines that become computers, Operations and industrial
  programs. [With J's Computers](computers.md)
- Plant Drawings by sections, with models, system diagrams and safety data sheets. [Manuals](manuals.md)
- About sixty advancements. [Advancements](advancements.md)
- The sounds of industry. [Sound](sound.md)
- The API for add-ons. [The API](api.md)
- The settings of every consequence. [Implementation](implementation.md)
