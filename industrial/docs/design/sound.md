# Sound

J's Industrial sounds like real industry: machines, power, air, alarms. The sounds are real recordings chosen to fit
(generating a sound is the last resort), and each is in the repository's asset registry with its source and its licence.

## What exists today

No sounds yet. The groups below are declared (`IndustrialSoundGroup`), each mixed in one of J's Core's channels
(machines, power, air and fluids, science in the Machines channel; safety in Alerts; the Clean Room and Transcendence
in Ambience), with the two rules: a machine drawing more power sounds louder, and an alarm carries far. The sounds
come with their machines, in a pass of their own.

**Sounds obey physics:**

- in the Aleph, **pitch follows the local time**: in a slow time pocket sounds turn low and slow, in a fast one high,
  and slow light gives a **Doppler** shift to moving beings, through J's Core's physics kit ([The Aleph](aleph.md));
- **a machine drawing more power sounds louder**, and the **Muffler** silences it ([Machines](machines.md));
- **alarms carry far**, to warn in time.
