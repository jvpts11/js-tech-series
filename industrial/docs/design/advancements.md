# Advancements

J's Industrial's advancements take their names from the real history of engineering and science: quotes, events and
jokes most players will know, all of them public.

## What exists today

None.

## To build

About sixty advancements, in four tabs, each with its own root:

| Tab | What they are about |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | T0 to T2: the first machines, power, steel, chemistry |
| Industrialisation | T3 to T5: multiblocks, the grid, the Clean Room, nuclear power |
| Discovery | research and accelerators, one for each major discovery |
| Transcendence | T6 to T9 and the Aleph |
