# Tools and armour

Tools and armour by material, utility tools, powered tools, protective suits, exo-suits and the modules that improve
them.

J's Industrial's tools and armour are for **industrial work**: mining, surviving dangerous places, working on machines.
Weapons are J's Warfare's. They **are not enchanted** at vanilla's table: they are improved with physical **modules**
that go in and come out. Vanilla tools and armour keep working, with no nerf.

## What exists today

Nothing.

### Powered tools

**Electric tools take an item battery that goes in and out**, as real cordless tools do: the batteries of
[Energy](energy.md) (lead-acid, nickel-cadmium, lithium-ion, solid-state) and the Antimatter Cell at the end. A spent
battery is swapped and charged in the Battery Charger.

| Tool | Tier | What it does | In real life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electric Drill | T2 | mines as a pickaxe, much faster | Fein, 1895 |
| Chainsaw | T2 | cuts the **whole tree** at once | Stihl's electric one, 1929 |
| Demolition Hammer | T3 | mines **3 by 3** ahead | |
| Arc Welder | T3 | welds: a tool in the recipes of steel structures, and it **repairs** metal machines, as an alternative to the Repair Kit | |
| Plasma Cutter | T4 | cuts metal: it breaks machines and metal blocks fast | the 1960s |
| Mining Laser | T5 | mines **from a distance** with a fibre laser beam | laser rock drilling, in research |

**Pneumatic tools** run on the compressed air of [Machines](machines.md): they carry an **air tank** filled at an **Air
Station**, an outlet of the Compressed Air Line.

| Tool | Tier | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pneumatic Jackhammer | T2 | mines **3 by 3**, stronger than the electric tool of its tier; real jackhammers are pneumatic |
| Impact Wrench | T2 | the Wrench, much faster |
| Rock Drill | T2 | drills holes for blasting charges |

**Why both families, as in real life:** electric tools **spark**, and a spark in a cloud of flammable gas makes it
**explode** ([Fluids and gases](fluids-and-gases.md)), as firedamp does in coal mines. Pneumatic tools **don't spark**,
which is why real mines use them; in a gassy mine, they are the safe choice.

**With fuel:**

- **Thermal Lance** (T3): iron rods burning in **oxygen** at 4,000 °C cut **any block**, even obsidian; it spends oxygen
  from a Gas Cylinder and rods. The real thermic lance cuts concrete and steel.
- **Oxy-Acetylene Torch** (T2): cuts and welds with the acetylene of [Chemistry](chemistry.md).

Experience comes out normally of everything mined with these tools: technology doesn't punish the player.

### Protective suits

Each piece of gear protects from what it protects from in real life, and not from everything:

| Gear | Tier | Protects from | Doesn't protect from | In real life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gas Mask | T2 | **toxic gases**, filtering them with activated carbon | **asphyxiants**: no filter gives oxygen | the masks of the First World War |
| SCBA | T3 | **toxic gases and asphyxiants**, with its own air in a cylinder, for as long as the cylinder lasts | | firefighters' breathing apparatus |
| Proximity Suit | T3 | **heat, fire, molten metal and lava**, for a few seconds of contact | | the aluminised suits of foundries and airport firefighters |
| Arc Flash Suit | T3 | the **arc flash** of a machine blowing two classes above ([Energy](energy.md)) | the shock of touching a wire: that is the gloves' job | electricians' arc-rated clothing (NFPA 70E) |
| Lineman's Gloves | T1, T3, T4 | **shock**, by class | | |
| Cryogenic Gear | T4 | **cryogenic liquids**: gloves and face shield | | the gear of whoever handles liquid nitrogen |
| Hazmat Suit and its successors | T3 to T5 | radioactive **contamination**, and contact with **acids and toxics** | external radiation | |
| Lead Apron | T2 | a little external radiation | | |
| Clean Room Suit | T4 | it **protects the room from the wearer**, not the wearer | | the "bunny suit" of chip fabs |
| Law Stabilizer and Full Stabilizer Suit | T8, T9 | the **dissonance** of the Aleph | | |

The radiation suits are described in [Nuclear](nuclear.md), the Clean Room Suit in [Clean Room](clean-room.md), the
stabilizers in [The Aleph](aleph.md). Suits take the armour slots and protect little against blows; protection and
strength come together in the exo-suits, through modules. Space suits are J's Space's, and diving gear J's Oceanics'.

### Exo-suits

| Exo-suit | Tier | What it is | In real life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hydraulic Exoframe | T3 | a heavy **hydraulic** frame: great strength (mining and breaking faster, pushing) and **slow**; no flight | General Electric's **Hardiman**, 1965, which lifted 680 kg |
| Industrial Exo-Suit | T4 | electric servomotors, an item battery | today's industrial exoskeletons (Sarcos) |
| Advanced Exo-Suit | T5 | lighter and stronger, on solid-state batteries | |
| Transcendent Exo-Suit | T7 | **genevium** plating, an **Antimatter Cell**, the **Law Stabilizer** built in | the series' bet |

**The pieces:**

- **helmet:** a **HUD** (the state of the machine looked at, radiation, temperature, dissonance; with J's Computers, the
  network's notifications too) and night vision;
- **chest:** **pockets** (more inventory) and the **jetpack**;
- **legs:** no weight penalty, and a sprint;
- **boots:** fall damping, assisted jumps, **magnetic adhesion** (walking on metal walls) and stepping up blocks by
  themselves.

**The jetpack**, by real history: **turbojets** burning **kerosene** from the refinery at T4 (as the JB-10 of 2015),
**electric ducted fans** on the battery at T5, the Antimatter Cell at T7 with no practical limit. It flies in two modes:
**free flight**, as an Elytra with thrust, and **vertical**, hovering to work on tall multiblocks.

**The look.** The Advanced and the Transcendent exo-suits look like **real powered armour**: armour plates, a closed
helmet with a **reflective visor**, broad shoulders and chest, the feel of a walking tank. The Advanced one is
steel-grey with industrial orange and a dark blue visor; the Transcendent one takes genevium's colour, with a visor that
**changes colour with the dissonance** around it.

**Module slots:** 2 per piece of passive armour, 3 at T4, 4 at T5, 5 at T7.

The **servomotors** are generic components of J's Core's catalogue ([Materials](materials.md)), with their industrial
recipe in J's Industrial; J's Robotics' robots and J's Transport's vehicles use the same ones.

### Modules

**For tools**

| Module | Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ore Sorter | T3 | +1 ore per block, up to 3: it sorts ore from waste as it breaks |
| Precision Head | T3 | the block comes out whole |
| Overclocked Motor | T2 | +25% speed, up to 4 |
| Self-Repair | T5 | spends energy to repair durability |
| Extended Reach | T4 | +1 block of reach, up to 2 |
| Vein Miner | T4 | breaks the **whole vein** of the same ore joined to the block |
| Spark Arrestor | T2 | an electric tool **stops sparking**, as real tools for explosive atmospheres |

**For armour**

| Module | Tier | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radiation Shielding | T3 | less **external** radiation, up to 4 |
| Rebreather | T2 | breathing under water |
| Heat Shield | T3 | fire and lava for a few seconds |
| Shock Absorbers | T2 | cancels falls up to 5 blocks, up to 3 (15 blocks) |
| Magnetic Boots | T4 | walking on metal walls |
| Filter Mask | T2 | a head piece filters **toxic gases**, as the gas mask |
| Air Supply | T3 | its own air, as the SCBA |
| Insulation | T3 | protects from **shock**, as the gloves, by class |
| Law Stabilizer | T8 | the bubble of our laws in the Aleph |
| Clean Room Seal | T4 | the armour counts as a clean room suit: the exo-suit can walk into the Clean Room |

An exo-suit with the right modules does the work of several protective suits: that is the reward of getting there. A
simple suit stays the cheap answer of the early game. With J's Space installed, a space module lets an exo-suit be worn
in space.
