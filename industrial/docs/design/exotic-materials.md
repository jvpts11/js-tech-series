# Exotic materials

Transcendence, T6 to T9: where the stable superheavy elements come from, the exotic forms of matter, antimatter, the
circuits and machines of the last tiers, and the portals. The dimension beyond the last portal has a page of its own,
[The Aleph](aleph.md).

This is where J's Industrial goes beyond today's science. It keeps one rule: **real physics as far as it reaches, and
from there the series' bet, always built on that physics.**

## What exists today

Nothing: the elements beyond 118 and the exotic forms of matter are planned in [Materials](materials.md).

**The Deep Core Miner** (T6) drills **through the bedrock**, below anything that can be dug. The deepest real borehole
(Kola, 12 km) and Project Mohole (1961), which tried to reach the mantle, never left the crust; from there on, it is the
series' bet. It spends the heaviest drilling mud (caesium formate, [Chemistry](chemistry.md)), **diamond bits** from the
HPHT Press and **cooling**, since the deeper, the hotter. It brings up **core fragments**, processed (ground, leached,
electrolysed) into **nickel-iron**, the stuff of the core; **platinum, iridium, osmium and gold**, since most of the
Earth's gold and platinum really sank into the core when the planet formed, iron-loving as they are; and traces of
**lehmannium**. It is the rich source of platinum metals at the end of the game.

what it gathers from dead stars; J's Industrial gives them their industrial recipes.

### Antimatter

In all of history CERN has made a few **nanograms**, kept in **Penning traps** (magnetic and electric fields in vacuum).
Making antimatter really costs a billion times more energy than it gives back, and **1 milligram**, annihilated,
releases **180 GJ** (E = mc²).

- **Making it** (T6): the **Antimatter Factory**, a multiblock with a proton synchrotron, a target, a **decelerator**
  (as CERN's AD and ELENA) and a positron source, cools antihydrogen and traps it. It is far more efficient than the
  real one (the series' bet), but still gives back only part of the energy it costs. **Anti-nucleosynthesis** in the
  same factory builds heavier anti-elements, each one dearer than the last (antihelium-4 was really observed, at RHIC,
  in 2011).
- **Keeping it:** the **Antimatter Trap** (a Penning trap), J's Core's containment in its form for antimatter, spends
  energy; without it, it stops taking more and releases nothing.
- **Counted in real units**, as amounts of anti-atoms (like a chemist's mole). Annihilation turns all the mass of both
  sides into energy, so **the heavier the anti-element, the more energy per amount**: antihelium 4 times antihydrogen,
  anticarbon 12 times, anti-iron 56 times, antilead 207 times. It is exact physics.
- **The Antimatter Cell** (T6 and T7) is the densest storage of the game, **1 mg = 180 GJ (50 MWh)** in a hand-held
  cell, for the armour and tools of the end ([Tools and armour](tools-and-armour.md)) and J's Space's antimatter
  engines.
- **The Antimatter Chamber** (T7) makes Singularity circuits with controlled micro-explosions.
- **The Antimatter Reactor** (T7, a large multiblock of variable size) annihilates antimatter with matter in a magnetic
  nozzle; the gamma rays heat a **tungsten** blanket that feeds the steam chain or the sCO₂ Turbine: **tens of GW**, by
  the anti-element and the size ([Energy](energy.md)).
- **A free source**, so antimatter is a source and not only a battery: the **Schwinger Field Generator** (T7) makes an
  electric field so strong it **pulls particle-antiparticle pairs out of the vacuum**, a real prediction of physics (the
  Schwinger effect) never reached; from it on, the factory gives more than it spends. With J's Space installed, the
  **antiproton belt**: in 2011 the PAMELA satellite found antiprotons trapped around the Earth, and NASA studies have
  proposed harvesting them. Before the T7, antimatter is a battery.

**The Singularity Reactor** (T7, 50 GW) feeds **any matter**, even waste, into a **micro black hole**: falling, matter
gives up to **40% of its mass as energy**, as the accretion discs of quasars (the theoretical limit for a spinning black
hole), the most powerful source in real physics. The black hole is made with a **Kugelblitz**: light concentrated until
it collapses, a real theoretical idea of a black hole made of energy alone, here from free-electron lasers and a pulse
of antimatter in the **Kugelblitz Ignitor** (below). Dark matter is a better fuel than ordinary matter. A natural micro
black hole captured in [The Aleph](aleph.md) feeds the reactor with no ignitor.

**The Microgravity Chamber** (T6): with no gravity, crystals grow without flaws, as the ones made on the ISS (ZBLAN
fibre). Gravity can't be switched off on Earth, but things can be **levitated with magnets**: diamagnetic levitation is
real (a frog was levitated at 16 tesla in 1997); here it is done with **teslium** magnets. **While it works, gravity is
off inside it**: players, mobs and items float, and a player inside moves as astronauts do on the ISS; when it stops,
gravity comes back and what floated falls. With J's Space installed, an orbital laboratory does the same.

**T9**

- **The Heart of the Universe** (a gigantic multiblock). In 1987 Edward Farhi and **Alan Guth** (the father of cosmic
  inflation) published a serious paper on **creating a universe in a laboratory**: a bubble of **false vacuum**, with
  enough energy, inflates into a new universe that parts from ours, and from outside looks like a micro black hole. The
  Heart creates and holds a **baby universe** in such a bubble, **burning aeternium** to seed and feed it, and draws
  energy from its inflation, the greatest source physics imagines: for all purposes **endless energy**, as much as the
  cables carry (the Singularity Cable has no ceiling). Its central sphere is **transparent**, and inside it the baby
  universe evolves, its time hugely sped up: a flash, then fog, then **galaxies forming**. It has no accidents: without
  aeternium or containment, the bubble parts from our universe and the energy stops.
- **The Creation Forge** (one of the largest multiblocks of the game). All the matter of the real universe was born from
  the energy of inflation, as it turned into particles ("reheating"). The Forge does the same, built in **concentric
  rings** around a **Reheating Core**:
  - the **Containment Rings**, coils that hold the core, are of **teslium** (T6), **hawkingium** (T7), **zwickium** (T8)
    or **cantorium** (T9): the rings' material sets what the Forge makes and how fast, as GregTech's coils;
  - **Catalyst Injectors** spend an exotic catalyst on each batch: time crystals, strange matter, or the core of a
    Sentient Singularity's planet for the dearest recipes;
  - ports for energy (with its class), matter and exotic matter, and the **link to the Heart of the Universe**;
  - **without the Heart**, on plenty of energy (the Dark Energy Collector), it works slowly and dearly: that is how the
    **first aeternium** is made, which then lights the Heart; **with the Heart linked**, it uses the baby universe's
    matter and runs far faster and cheaper;
  - it makes **Omniversal circuits**, **aeternium**, the **exotic alloys** of superheavy elements for the last machines
    and armour, and **any element out of nothing**, slowly;
  - it processes **in parallel and in huge batches**, more as its rings grow, and its rings **spin** around a core of
    white plasma while the sky around it darkens.
- **The Reality Projector** (a large multiblock): a **projection chamber** at its centre, a column of light where the
  object takes shape, surrounded by **emitter arrays**; the more emitters, the faster it replicates. It materialises
  matter from data, gives cantorium, newtonium and turingium, and **replicates any item from its record**:
  - the **Matter Scanner** (T9), a reading chamber set against it, takes an item apart **atom by atom** as it reads it,
    and writes its **record**: in a **Pattern Crystal** of time crystal without J's Computers, as a file on the network
    with it;
  - the cost follows the item's **recipe**, multiplied, not its mass (by mass, a kilogram would cost 90 PJ): replicating
    always costs more than making with the factory built, but spares the factory;
  - **not replicated**: aeternium, antimatter, exotic matter and Antimatter Cells, the Sentient Singularity's planets
    and the Aleph's other unique things, and living beings, so no loop makes energy or matter from nothing;
  - the object appears **layer by layer** in a beam of light, as a 3D print made of atoms.

### Portals

- **Quantum Link** (T5): blocks in **pairs**; what goes into one comes out of the other (items, fluids, gases, energy
  and heat), at any distance and **between dimensions**. It can't be walked through: it is logistics only. It is cheap
  to run. Real entanglement **carries neither matter nor information** (the no-communication theorem): this is open
  fantasy, and the manual says so.
- **Wormhole Gate** (T8): two gates join two places of the same world or of different worlds (the Nether, the End, and
  with J's Space installed, the planets). It is **walked through** by players, mobs and vehicles, and carries everything
  in bulk. It is held open by **negative-mass matter**: Morris and Thorne showed in 1988 that a traversable wormhole
  needs exactly exotic matter with negative energy not to collapse. It spends energy and exotic matter while open, and
  closes without energy. Transcendent circuits are made beside an open gate.

### Settings

- **the Quantum Link exists**: off for whoever finds it too easy a shortcut;
- **wormholes cost nothing**: a gate stays open with no energy or exotic matter;
- **easy Wormhole Gate recipe**: a cheaper recipe, so the gate comes earlier.
