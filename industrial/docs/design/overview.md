# Overview

J's Industrial is the industry of the J's Tech Series: from the first plate beaten by hand to machines that make matter
out of a baby universe. It follows the real history of industry and science, tier by tier, from T0 to T9, and goes
beyond it only where science itself ends.

It is one of the main mods of the series, with J's Computers, J's Space, J's Transport and J's Warfare, and it is played
on its own: nothing in it needs another mod of the series ([The series](series.md)).

## Three phases

| Phase | Tiers | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | T0 to T2 | electricity, grinding, basic metallurgy, petrochemistry; conveyors and fluids; plastics and the first silicon |
| Industrialisation | T3 to T5 | steel and multiblocks, alloys, the Clean Room, nuclear power, nanotechnology |
| Transcendence | T6 to T9 | quantum fabrication, antimatter, other universes, making things from nothing |

A tier is defined by the circuit the player can make ([Tiers and circuits](tiers-and-circuits.md)).

## Principles

**Real history, real names, real units.** Each tier has the machines of its time, with their real names and the year
they were born; energy is in joules, watts and volts, fluids in cubic metres, chemistry in real reactions with their
real proportions. The player learns, along the way, how the real world is built.

**Circuits are the progression; research is a second gate.** Making a tier's circuit opens the tier. Research only gates
frontier science, as real discoveries in their real order ([Science](science.md)).

**Nothing is hidden.** A recipe can be locked for running, never for seeing: JEI, EMI and the manuals always show it,
with the discovery it needs. A player can always plan.

**Real consequences, each with its setting.** Overvoltage burns machines, pipes corrode, gases leak and explode, boilers
blow, reactors melt down. Each consequence has its setting, so a server can turn it off; there are no settings that turn
a part of the mod off as a "module" ([Implementation](implementation.md)).

**Transcendence has no accidents.** Power sources a player builds to forget, from fusion up, simply stop when something
fails; containment of exotic matter stops taking more and releases nothing.

**Things connect physically.** What serves many machines reaches each one through a pipe, a cable or a tube, never by a
radius around a block. Effects over an area exist only as immersion: the Microgravity Chamber's zero gravity, the
Aleph's laws.

**Many machines, or fewer upgraded ones.** Upgrades make a machine faster or thriftier, rarely both; spreading a factory
out costs room and materials, packing it tight costs energy. Both are fair ways to play ([Machines](machines.md)).

**Real physics as far as it reaches.** Past what science knows, the series bets, and always on real physics: the island
of stability, the Casimir effect, baby universes, the multiverse.
