# The Aleph

A dimension where nothing makes sense: rocks float, time runs at other speeds, space folds on itself, and life is made
of light and of four dimensions.

It has a real idea behind it. Cosmology's **level II multiverse** (Tegmark), and the "landscape" of string theory,
predict **universes with different physical constants**: another gravity, another speed of light, another time. A
wormhole torn far enough falls into one of them. The name is the aleph of Cantor, the symbol of the infinities, and the
point of Borges' story that holds every point of the universe.

The Aleph is the natural home of exotic matter ([Exotic materials](exotic-materials.md)), and its danger is physics
itself.

## What exists today

Nothing.

## To build

### Getting in and getting back

- The Aleph lies **on the other side of a wormhole taken too far**. Forcing a gate beyond our universe, into a vacuum
  with other laws, takes a gate of its own, the **Aleph Gate** (T8). It needs the discovery **Other Vacua**
  ([Science](science.md)), from the data of dark energy and Casimir experiments, the hints that the vacuum could be
  another, and **a lot of negative-mass matter** to stay open.
- It is a deliberate act: whoever never builds it never sees the Aleph, and the rest of the game stays the same. A
  server setting turns the dimension off.
- The gate always opens onto the same **landing region** of the Aleph in each world, a fairly calm shore where the laws
  are **almost** ours. The further out, the stranger.
- On the other side a way back forms while the Aleph Gate is open, and the gate stays open **while it has energy** at
  home. If it closes with a player inside, the **Aleph Tether**, an item made before going in, pulls the player back to
  the gate as it is spent. With materials of the Aleph, a way back can also be built inside.
- With J's Computers installed, a probe could go first, with no player, and send data back.

### Laws by region

The Aleph is divided into **regions of laws**, larger than biomes and laid over them. Each region has its own values for
a set of laws. Near the landing they are almost ours, and they grow stranger with distance.

| Law | How it can vary | What it feels like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gravity | stronger or weaker, **zero**, **upwards**, **sideways**, or **towards a point** (a mini-planet where one walks around a rock) | huge jumps, slow falls, walking on walls, floating rocks |
| Time | **slower** or **faster** than ours, in pockets (real relativity's time dilation) | the world around in slow motion or fast-forward; fire burns slowly, water rushes |
| Light | slower (colours **shift with movement**, as in the Doppler effect), bent (**mirages**, as in gravitational lenses), darkness with no source, glow with no sun | seeing what is behind, seeing a region twice |
| Space | **bigger inside than outside**, regions that **wrap around** (walking east, one comes back from the west), shortcuts between far points | maps that don't add up, getting lost |
| Matter | water **flows upwards**, fire is cold, sand floats, explosions are slow | what one knows of the game stops holding |
| Electromagnetism | machines and cables from home work worse or better; magnets repel instead of attracting | building there is an engineering challenge |

- The **Law Probe** (T8) measures the laws where the player stands (gravity, the speed of time, the speed of light, and
  whatever else varies), and shows them on screen, to plan before building.
- **In a fast time pocket, machines work faster.** It is the place, not a block; it is the great reason to build in the
  Aleph.
- These physics live in J's Core as a kit any mod can use, for visual effects and mechanics alike: gravity fields, time
  rates, light effects, non-Euclidean space, curvature around masses.

### Singularities

Natural **micro black holes**, from the size of a block to the size of a **tree**:

- they look like real ones: a black sphere, a **ring of bent light** around it (an Einstein ring) and, on the ones
  feeding, a glowing **accretion disc**;
- they **pull** players, mobs, items and loose blocks, more the closer they are, and one can **fall into orbit**; they
  **slow time** around them, as relativity says;
- the **event horizon** forgives nothing: what goes in is gone, a danger one sees and avoids, as lava;
- small ones **evaporate** through **Hawking radiation**: the smaller, the hotter and the shorter-lived; the tiniest
  flicker and vanish in a flash, the big ones stay;
- the **Singularity Containment** (T8) captures a natural one and takes it home, where it feeds the Singularity Reactor
  with no Kugelblitz Ignitor ([Exotic materials](exotic-materials.md)).

### Biomes

The Aleph has the shape of a world, as the Overworld, but its regions of laws draw it. From the calmest to the
strangest:

| Biome | What it is like |
| --- | --- |
| Shores of Arrival | the landing: laws almost ours, pale stone, the first strange plants |
| Floating Archipelago | islands of **negative-mass rock** floating at every height, weak gravity |
| Inverted Highlands | gravity pulls upwards: the land **hangs from the sky** and is walked on its underside |
| Crystal Groves | forests of **time crystals** that tick like clocks, with fast and slow time pockets |
| Fractal Peaks | mountains that repeat themselves (the Mandelbrot set), detail without end |
| Lens Plains | light bends: mirages, a doubled horizon, and other places of the Aleph in the sky |
| The Fold | a region that **wraps around**: walking east, one arrives from the west |
| Escher Ruins | ruins of impossible architecture (Penrose stairs, looping corridors) left by whoever built them |
| Singularity Fields | micro black holes scattered about, slow time, bent light |
| Dark Fog Basin | a fog of **dark matter**, with almost no light |
| The Deep Strange | the farthest and most dangerous region, of **strange matter**, where the laws can hardly be recognised |

Each biome has its own rock, plants and sounds.

**The Builders' technology**, artefacts studied in the CRC and turned into machines:

- **Tesseract Crate**: a crate **bigger inside than outside**; one block holds what a warehouse would. It is one crate,
  not networked storage (that is J's Computers');
- **Fold Gate**: the Fold's shortcuts brought home: two small gates join two nearby points with no wormhole, a short and
  cheap teleport;
- **Gravity Plate**: a block that **changes the gravity above it** (sideways, upwards, zero), for lifts, tracks and
  effects, built on J's Core's physics kit;
- **Time Pocket Generator**: a small **fast time pocket** around one machine, at a very high energy cost; the final
  reward of the Builders.

### Dissonance

A player's body comes with **our** laws. Where the laws are others, that difference builds up, as radiation does: this
is **dissonance**, a hazard of J's Core's hazard mechanic, next to radiation.

- It builds up **faster the further the local laws are from ours**; the Law Probe shows that **departure** as a number.
  At the landing it hardly rises; in the Deep Strange it climbs fast.
- It falls back in normal laws, or at home.

| Level | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| I | sight **warps** and colours shift |
| II | the player's own gravity **drifts** (sliding, floating a little) and sounds change pitch |
| III | time **stutters**: the player's moves catch and skip now and then, and hunger rises faster |
| IV | the player starts to **fold**: small involuntary jumps through space, and slow damage |
| V | heavy damage; staying is dying |

**Protection**

- **Law Stabilizer** (T8, an armour module): **a bubble of our laws around the body**. Inside it the player walks with
  our gravity and our time, even on ground of inverted gravity. It slows dissonance a lot and **spends energy**: an
  Antimatter Cell suits it ([Tools and armour](tools-and-armour.md)).
- **Full Stabilizer Suit** (T9): immunity.
- Without energy, the stabilizer turns off, and dissonance starts counting.

**Building there:** a **Law Anchor** (T8, a block) makes **a zone of our laws** around a base in the Aleph, where one
can live and work as at home. But inside it there is no fast time either: whoever wants accelerated factories builds
them outside, with stabilizers.

A server setting turns dissonance off.
