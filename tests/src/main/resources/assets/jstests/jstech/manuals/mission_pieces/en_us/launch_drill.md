# Launch drill

## What it is

A page of the test mod laid out in the Mission Handbook, holding every piece a mission's document draws: the boxed heads, a caution, a checklist with a line the revision changed, a warning and two cards on rings.

> [!CAUTION]
> No launch in a thunderstorm: the panel holds the count.

## How to use it

- [ ] Rocket ... on pad
- [ ] Hoses ... connected
- [!] Cryo tanks ... venting
- [ ] Countdown ... start
- [ ] T-0 ... go
- [ ] Hoses ... away
- [ ] Climb ... in sight

## What can go wrong

> [!WARNING]
> No flame trench and no deluge: the launch wrecks the pad and what is around it.

