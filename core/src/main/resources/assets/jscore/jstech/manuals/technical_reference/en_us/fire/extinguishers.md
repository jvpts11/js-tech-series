# Fire Extinguishers

## What it is

Red cylinders you spray on a fire, each of one agent, its band in the colour of the European code: water all red, foam cream, carbon dioxide black, dry powder blue.

## What it is for

Putting a fire out before it spreads, with the agent that suits what burns.

> [!DEFINE] Class of fire
> What burns: solids (wood, cloth), flammable liquids, electrical (something live with current), metals (sodium, magnesium) and cooking oils. Each takes its own agent.

## How to get it

In creative, take them from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need them give them their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Hold use and aim at the fire: it sprays a cone ahead, water farthest and carbon dioxide the shortest. Its bar shows what is left.
2. Water puts out solids; foam solids, liquids and cooking oils; carbon dioxide liquids and electrical fires, and leaves wood to smoulder; dry powder all but cooking oils.
3. Use a water extinguisher on water to fill it again.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Spraying an electrical fire hurts you.
> Water and foam carry the current: use carbon dioxide or dry powder.

> [!PROBLEM] The fire flares up round where you sprayed.
> Water spreads a burning liquid or oil: use foam.

> [!PROBLEM] The fire bursts.
> Water on a burning metal makes it burst: only dry powder smothers it.

> [!PROBLEM] The fire lights again.
> Carbon dioxide leaves wood smouldering: finish it with water.
