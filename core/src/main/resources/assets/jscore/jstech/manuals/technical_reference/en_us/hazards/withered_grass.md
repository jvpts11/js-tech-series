# Withered Grass

## What it is

Grass killed by contamination: where radiation or a pollution is high, grass dies, flowers and saplings wither to dead bushes and crops stop growing.

## What it is for

A warning you can see from afar: the land is fouled here.

## How to get it

It forms by itself where the land is contaminated; break it with silk touch to keep it, or it gives dirt.

## How to use it

1. Clean the land, or wait for the contamination to fall: the grass grows back slowly.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The grass keeps dying.
> The land is still contaminated: find what fouls it with a Geiger counter or the hazard map.
