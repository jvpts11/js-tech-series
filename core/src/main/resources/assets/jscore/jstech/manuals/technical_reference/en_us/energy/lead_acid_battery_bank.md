# Lead-Acid Battery Bank

## What it is

The basic battery: cells of lead plates in acid, in a steel case, at low voltage.

## What it is for

Keeping energy for when nothing makes it, and smoothing what a line carries.

Table: What it keeps

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Store | 50 MJ |
| Takes and gives | 10 kW, 500 J a tick |
| Voltage | Low, 400 V |

## How to get it

Craft it of four iron ingots at the corners, a copper ingot above and below a water bucket, and redstone at its sides. With J's Industrial installed, it is that mod's first energy bank, made with its own recipe.

## How to use it

1. Place it on a line: it takes what the line has spare and gives it back when the line runs short.
2. The gauge on its sides shows how full it is, in four steps.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It neither charges nor gives.
> It takes and gives through any face, but only by a line it is plugged into; lay a cable to it.
