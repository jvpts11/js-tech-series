# Hazard Map

## What it is

A handheld map of the land round you, each chunk coloured by the hazard read there: radiation, or a pollution.

## What it is for

Seeing where it is safe to go, and where you measured last and how long ago.

> [!DEFINE] Chunk
> A column of the world 16 blocks wide. The map knows only the chunks an instrument in your hands or a sensor on a network read; the rest stays grey.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Hold it: it shows on your screen, live, with what is read here.
2. Use it to open the whole map. Pick the hazard with the buttons at the top; the legend says what each colour means. Click a chunk for its reading and its age; drag to look further.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The map is all grey.
> Nothing was measured here yet: walk the land with a Geiger counter in your hotbar.
