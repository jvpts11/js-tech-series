# Basic Wrench

## What it is

An open-ended spanner of iron, the series' shared wrench.

## What it is for

Setting how each end of a pipe or a line of items meets the block it plugs into.

Table: The modes of an end

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | As the block says: a machine's output gives, its input takes, a tank both |
| Push | The line only fills the block, and never draws from it |
| Pull | The line only draws from the block, and never fills it |
| Off | The end plugs into nothing, and joins no cable beside it |

## How to get it

Craft it of four iron ingots: two at the top corners, two down the middle.

## How to use it

1. Use it on the end of a wire, near the block it meets, to go round the modes.
2. Sneak to open the end's screen, on a pipe or a line of items: there the mode, the priority, the rate, the channel and the filter are set.
3. On any other line, sneak to go round the modes the other way.
4. The line says which end it set and how, above the hotbar.
5. While it is in your hand, what flows along the wire you look at, which way and how much, shows beside the crosshair.
6. On a machine, use it on a face to go round what that face does with the resource it shows: in, out, both or neither. While it is in your hand, each face of the machine you look at shows in its colour: blue in, amber out, green both, grey neither.
7. Sneak and turn the mouse wheel at a machine to show another of its resources.
8. Sneak and use it on a machine to take the machine away whole: its contents, upgrades, fuse, battery, settings and the work under way go with it. A pickaxe breaks it instead, and what it held falls out.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Nothing changes.
> Point at the wire itself, near the end you want; a part on the face closes it.

> [!PROBLEM] A machine neither changes nor comes away.
> It is someone else's: only its owner and whom they trust may set it up or take it.
