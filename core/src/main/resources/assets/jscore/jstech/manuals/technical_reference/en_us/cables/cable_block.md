# The cable block

## What it is

One block that every mod of the series lays its cables in. Each kind of cable runs in a lane of its own, so cables of different mods can share a block without touching.

## What it is for

Running energy, data and the computers' peripheral cables through the same space, each kept to its own line.

## How to get it

Take a cable from a mod's creative tab: J's Industrial's Energy Cable, J's Computers' data and peripheral cables.

## How to use it

1. Place a cable like a block.
2. Place a cable of another kind in the same block: it takes its own lane.
3. Break the block to take its cables back, each one as its own item.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Two cables side by side do not join.
> Only cables of the same line join. An energy cable never joins a data cable, even in one block.
