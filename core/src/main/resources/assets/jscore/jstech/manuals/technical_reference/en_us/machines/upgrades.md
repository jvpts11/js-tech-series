# Machine Upgrades

## What it is

Real parts put into a machine's upgrade slots: motors, a drive, extra lanes, recovery units, tooling, a muffler and a separator. They are never used up. Any machine of the series with upgrade slots takes them.

Table: What each does

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Brushed, Induction and Brushless Motor | +50% speed, +75% energy; up to 4 motors in a machine, of any kinds |
| Variable Frequency Drive | -20% energy; up to 4 |
| Parallel Line | One more recipe at once, its energy in proportion; up to 4 |
| Advanced Parallel Line | Two more recipes at once, their energy in proportion; up to 4 |
| Gas Recovery Unit | -15% of the gases a recipe spends; up to 4 |
| Solvent Recovery Unit | -15% of the liquids a recipe spends |
| Precision Tooling | +10% of what the machine makes; up to 2 |
| Muffler | The machine is silent |
| Byproduct Separator | +50% byproducts |

Gains add up and savings multiply: four motors make a machine three times as fast, and four drives leave it at 41% of its energy.

## What it is for

Making a machine faster, thriftier, quieter or more productive. Slots are few, so a machine is fast or thrifty, hardly both: whoever wants both builds more machines.

## How to get it

Craft each at the Crafting Table from iron, copper, gold and bronze ingots and the game's redstone, quartz, glass panes and diamond; the Advanced Parallel Line from two Parallel Lines.

## How to use it

1. Open the machine and its Upgrades tab.
2. Put the upgrades into its upgrade slots: the tooltip of each says what it does and how many fit in one machine.
3. The tab shows what the machine's upgrades do together.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] An upgrade does not go into the slot.
> That many of it already sit in the machine, or the machine has no upgrade slots.

> [!PROBLEM] The machine spends much more energy.
> Motors and lines cost energy for what they gain. A Variable Frequency Drive takes some of it back.
