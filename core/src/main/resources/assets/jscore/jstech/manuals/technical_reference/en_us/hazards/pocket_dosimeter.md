# Pocket Dosimeter

## What it is

A small meter you carry: it shows the dose of radiation your body has taken, and the band of radiation sickness it reached.

## What it is for

Knowing how much more you can take before you have to rest away from the sources.

> [!DEFINE] Sievert
> The unit of a dose, Sv. About one sievert brings a mild sickness, Rad I; eight and more come close to deadly, Rad V.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Carry it anywhere on you: it shows on your screen.
2. Use it to hold it up; LIGHT lights its face for the dark.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A lamp is lit.
> You reached a band of radiation sickness: get away from the sources, and your body heals slowly.
