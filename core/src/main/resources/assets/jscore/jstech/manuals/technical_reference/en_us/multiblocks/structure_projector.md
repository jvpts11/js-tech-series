# Structure Projector

## What it is

A projector that shows any multiblock of any mod you have in the world, before you build it: the blocks still missing as pale ghosts, a wrong block outlined in red, and above the structure how much is in place.

## What it is for

Building a multiblock right the first time, block by block, without counting layers from a picture.

> [!DEFINE] Ghost
> A pale, see-through block where a block of the multiblock still goes. Look at one to see which block and on which layer.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that build multiblocks give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place it beside where the multiblock is to stand: it faces you as you place it, and shows the multiblock on the side it faces.
2. Use it to open its screen and choose the multiblock from the list.
3. Turn it with the knob, mirror it if it allows, and show it a layer at a time with the layer's wheel, or only what is still missing.
4. Build into the ghosts: each goes as its block is placed, and the count above follows.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Nothing shows.
> Its switch is off, no multiblock is chosen, or you are more than 64 blocks away.

> [!PROBLEM] A block stays outlined in red.
> Another block stands there. Break it and place the one the ghost shows.
