# Network Probe

## What it is

A handheld instrument in an orange holster that reads a whole line at once, of any kind: energy, liquids, gases or items.

## What it is for

Finding what a line joins, what flows along it, where it is too narrow and what it loses on the way.

Table: Its pages

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Summary | The flow of the last minute against the narrowest cable, what the consumers want, the losses, and how many sources, consumers and stores it joins |
| Members | Every block it plugs into, by what it is to the line and by kind, with what each gives or takes |
| Flow | Each second of the last minute, with the most, the least and the mean |
| Losses | What the cables lost, against what the sources sent |

## How to get it

Take it from the creative tab J's Tech.

## How to use it

1. Use it on a wire of a line to link it to that line.
2. Use it in the air to hold it up and read its display; the keys under the display turn its pages.
3. On the members' page, choose a row with the pad and press OK: those blocks are outlined in the world, through walls, for ten seconds.
4. HOLD keeps the reading still, SHOW puts it beside the crosshair while you hold it, LIGHT lights the display and POWER switches it off.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The display says there is nothing to read.
> The wire it was linked to was taken out, or its chunk is far away; link it again.
