# Sprinkler

## What it is

A sprinkler head hung under a ceiling: its red glass bulb bursts at 68 °C, the heat of a fire below, and it showers the place under it.

## What it is for

Putting out a fire in a room by itself, while nobody is there.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place it under a ceiling and run a water pipe to it, or put a tank beside it: it holds a bucket and draws more from the pipe.
2. It showers a hundred millibuckets a second while it is open, and shuts half a minute after the heat is gone.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It opens but does nothing.
> It has no water: check its pipe.

> [!PROBLEM] The fire it showers flares up or bursts.
> Water is wrong for burning liquids, oils and metals: watch those with a detector instead.
