# Insulated Copper Cable

## What it is

The basic cable: a low voltage line of J's Energy, copper in black rubber, laid like every cable of the series in the shared cable block.

## What it is for

Carrying energy from what makes or keeps it to what spends it, block to block. While J's Energy is off, as it comes, it carries FE to any mod's machine.

Table: What it carries

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Voltage | Low, 400 V |
| Most it carries | 10 kW, 500 J a tick |
| What it loses | A tenth of what it carries over a hundred blocks |

## How to get it

Craft six of a row of copper ingots between two rows of dried kelp. With J's Industrial installed, it is that mod's first cable, made with its own recipe.

## How to use it

1. Lay it from a store or a generator to a machine.
2. A line longer than it needs loses more on the way: keep it short.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The machine at the end gets less than it needs.
> The line carries 10 kW at most, and loses some on the way; lay a second line, or keep it shorter.
