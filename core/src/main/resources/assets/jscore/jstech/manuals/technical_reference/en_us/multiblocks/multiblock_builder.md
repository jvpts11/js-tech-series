# Multiblock Builder

## What it is

A handheld instrument that builds a multiblock around its terminal for you, block by block, from your inventory: it says what it will place and how much of each you have, and asks what goes where a place takes several blocks.

## What it is for

Building a large multiblock without placing every block of it by hand.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that build multiblocks give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place the multiblock's terminal, then use the builder on it: it is aimed at it, and its display shows what is missing and what you have. Or use it on a Structure Projector showing the multiblock: it builds it where the projector shows it, terminal and all, which is the way for a terminal that stands off the ground.
2. On the page of choices, choose with the arrows what goes where a place takes several blocks.
3. Press OK: it first takes out whatever stands where the multiblock must stay empty or where another block goes, from the top down, and gives the blocks back to you; then it builds, a block at a time, as you would place them. HOLD holds it and carries it on.
4. SHOW shows the multiblock's ghosts while you hold it; LIGHT lights its display.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It says it aims at no multiblock it knows.
> The block is not a multiblock's terminal, or the projector shows nothing. Use it on the terminal itself, or choose the multiblock on the projector.

> [!PROBLEM] It stops with a reason.
> You ran out of a block, walked too far away, a place is protected, or something it cannot move is in the way, as bedrock. Its display says which; fix it and press OK again.
