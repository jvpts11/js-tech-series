# Technical Reference

This binder holds the reference for every mod of the series you have. Each has its chapter and its tab at the edge.

Every entry says, in this order, what the thing is, what it is for, how to get it, how to use it step by step, and what can go wrong. A figure (Figure 3-9) shows what a part looks like, a table (Table 3-7) gives its numbers, and a number after See (See 3.4.3) leads to another entry.

The magnifier on the top edge finds any part by name in the index. Hold the manual key over an item to open its page.
