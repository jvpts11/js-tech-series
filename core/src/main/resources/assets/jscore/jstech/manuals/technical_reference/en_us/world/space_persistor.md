# Space Persistor

## What it is

A chunk loader: it keeps an area round it loaded and working while nobody is near, so a quarry far away, a station or a starship left in orbit goes on.

## What it is for

Keeping machines working far from you, as far as you want to reach, within the number of chunks you may keep and the server's budget.

> [!DEFINE] Chunk
> A column of the world 16 blocks wide. The game loads the chunks near players and lets the rest go; a machine in a chunk let go stops.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place it: it is yours, and keeps a square of three by three chunks round it.
2. Use it to open its screen. The map shows the land round it, the chunks it keeps outlined, another owner's hatched; zoom out with the minus.
3. Pick a shape with the keys (a square, a circle, a line or only what you click) and turn the radius' drums to reach further.
4. Click a chunk on the map to add it, or take it off.
5. When the server makes it spend energy, feed it power; its Protection tab holds a fuse and a battery as every machine's does.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The FULL lamp is lit.
> You keep as many chunks as you may, or the server keeps as many as its budget allows. Take chunks off here or elsewhere.

> [!PROBLEM] The KEEPING lamp is dark.
> It is switched off, short of power, or has nothing to keep: feed it, switch it on, or check its area.
