# Exotic Matter Containment

## What it is

A heavy vessel whose field holds one exotic form of matter: neutronium, strange matter, dark matter and the rest, every one but antimatter.

## What it is for

Keeping the exotic matter the series' mods make or gather, which nothing else holds.

Table: What it holds and spends

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Holds | 1,000 mB of one material |
| Spends | 1 kW while it holds something |
| Energy | J's Energy at low voltage, or FE while J's Energy is off |

## How to get it

The mods that keep exotic matter in it, J's Industrial and J's Space, give it their recipes; in creative, take it from the J's Tech tab.

## How to use it

1. Lay a line to it and keep the energy coming: it spends it for as long as it holds something.
2. Fill and empty it by a line of exotic matter, from any side.
3. Broken, it keeps what it holds in the item it drops; placed again, it holds it again.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It takes nothing and gives nothing.
> It has no energy: sealed, it lets nothing out, and nothing is lost. Bring the energy back.
