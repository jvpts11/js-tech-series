# Basic Pipes

## What it is

Three pipes, each holding its block alone, with a sight glass in each block that shows what flows inside at its level, moving the way it goes.

## What it is for

Carrying liquids and gases from what makes them to what uses or keeps them.

Table: What each carries

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Copper Pipe | Liquids up to 200 °C, 100 mB a tick |
| Iron Gas Pipe | Gases at low pressure, 100 mB a tick |
| Vacuum-Jacketed Pipe | Liquids from the coldest up to 200 °C, 400 mB a tick |

## How to get it

Craft six of a row of glass panes between two rows of copper ingots, or of iron ingots for the gas pipe; four cryogenic pipes of iron, glass panes and obsidian. With J's Industrial installed, they are its first pipes, made with its own recipes.

## How to use it

1. Lay a pipe from a machine's output to another's input: the machine pushes what it makes along it.
2. Two tanks never pour into each other by themselves: set the end of the one to empty to pull, with the Basic Wrench.
3. Each end can be set apart: its mode, its priority, its rate, its channel and its filter.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A pipe bursts or cracks and spills.
> A liquid too hot or too cold for it reached it for a while. Use the pipe made for it.

> [!PROBLEM] Nothing flows.
> The pipe's end may be off, on another channel, or its filter may let nothing through.
