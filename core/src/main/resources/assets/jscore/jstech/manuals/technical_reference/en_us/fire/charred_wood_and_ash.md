# Charred Wood and Ash

## What it is

What fire leaves: logs and planks charred black, and a layer of ash where cloth, leaves and thin wood burnt away.

## What it is for

Charred wood burns no more; it builds as wood does. Ash shows where a fire went.

## How to get it

They form by themselves where a fire burns wood and cloth away; break them to keep them.

## How to use it

1. Build with charred wood as with any wood; dig ash with a shovel.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Burnt wood leaves nothing.
> The server turned off what fire leaves, in the balance settings.
