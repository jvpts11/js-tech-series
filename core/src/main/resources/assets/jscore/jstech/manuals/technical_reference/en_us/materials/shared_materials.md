# Materials

## What it is

What the world is made of, which every mod of the series shares: the elements and their isotopes, alloys, minerals, the exotic forms of matter, the common fuels and the generic components. A material comes in forms: an ingot, a dust, a plate, a wire, a gear, an ore in each rock, a liquid. Its tooltip gives its symbol and, with Shift held, more.

## What it is for

Every mod uses the same ones, so the iron plate one mod makes is the iron plate another asks for. Only the forms some installed mod uses are in the game, and later machines and parts are made from them.

![Iron Plate](item:jscore:iron_plate)

Table: The materials made today

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Iron Dust | From iron ore, raw iron or an ingot, in a Macerator |
| Iron Plate | From an iron ingot, in a Compressor |
| Copper Plate | From a copper ingot, in a Compressor |

## How to get it

Make them with the machines of J's Industrial, or take them from its creative tab, where they are shown.

## How to use it

1. Grind ore in a Macerator: one ore gives two dusts.
2. Smelt each dust in a furnace or an Electric Furnace: one dust gives one ingot.
3. Press ingots in a Compressor: one ingot gives one plate.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A dust will not go into a machine.
> Only the recipes the machine knows take it. Dust is smelted, not pressed.
