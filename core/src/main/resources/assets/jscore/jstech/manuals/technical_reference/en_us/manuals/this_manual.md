# This manual

## What it is

The Technical Reference: the binder that holds a chapter for every mod of the series you have, each with its tab at the edge.

## What it is for

Finding out what a thing is and how to use it, without leaving the game.

## How to get it

You are given one the first time you join a world. Another is in the creative tab J's Core.

## How to use it

1. Use it to open it at its cover; click to open it.
2. Click a line of the contents, a tab, or a number after See to go there.
3. Turn pages with the arrows, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel.
4. Use the magnifier on the top edge to search the index by name.
5. Hold the manual key over any item, in an inventory or in your hand, to open its page.
6. Press the sheet on the top edge to tear out a copy of the page on the left; in an item frame, the sheet shows its page.
7. Use it on a lectern to lay it there, for anyone who passes to read.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] An item opens no page.
> No manual has an entry for it yet.

> [!PROBLEM] A chapter is missing.
> Its mod is not installed: the binder holds the mods you have.
