# Clamp Meter

## What it is

A handheld instrument that reads energy. Clamped on a wire of a line, it reads what crosses the wire and which way; clamped on a machine or a store, what goes in and out of each of its faces.

## What it is for

Finding where the energy goes, how much a line carries against its ceiling, and which machine draws it.

Table: Its keys and its dial

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HOLD | Keeps the reading still |
| MAX MIN | Shows the most and the least that crossed in a tick since it was clamped |
| SHOW | Shows the reading beside the crosshair while it is in hand |
| LIGHT | Lights the display |
| Dial | Off, J's Energy's own units, or FE |

## How to get it

Take it from the creative tab J's Tech.

## How to use it

1. Use it on a cable to clamp it on the energy wire you aim at.
2. Use it on a machine or a store to read it face by face.
3. Hold it: its reading shows beside the crosshair.
4. Use it in the air to hold it up and read the details on its display.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It reads nothing.
> Its dial is off, or what it was clamped on is gone. Turn the dial, or clamp it again.

> [!PROBLEM] A line reads no direction.
> Nothing crossed the wire yet, or it was clamped a moment ago; wait a second.
