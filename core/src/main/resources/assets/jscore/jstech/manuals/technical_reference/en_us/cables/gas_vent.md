# Gas Vent

## What it is

The end of a gas line that lets out into the air whatever gas the line brings to the inlet on its front, all of it, puffing it from under its rain cap in the gas's colour.

## What it is for

Getting rid of a gas nobody wants, so the machine that makes it never stops full: the Air Separation Unit's nitrogen, or the chlorine of an Electrolytic Cell running without salt.

## How to get it

Craft it from six iron ingots round an Iron Gas Pipe, with iron bars on top where the gas goes out.

## How to use it

1. Place it with its front, the gas inlet, toward the gas pipe: it takes gas through its front alone.
2. Lay a gas pipe from the machine's gas outlet to the vent. Looked at, it says what it is letting out and how much a second.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Whoever stands near it coughs, chokes or is poisoned.
> What it lets out becomes a cloud of the gas above it that spreads round it before it thins: chlorine poisons, nitrogen and carbon dioxide leave too little air to breathe in a closed room, hydrogen burns from a flame. Put it in the open, away from where people work, or wear a gas mask.

> [!PROBLEM] Nothing goes out.
> The pipe may reach a side or the back of the vent, which take nothing; turn the vent's front toward it.
