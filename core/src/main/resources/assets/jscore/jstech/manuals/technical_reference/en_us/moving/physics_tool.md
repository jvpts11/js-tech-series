# Physics Tool

## What it is

A gunmetal tool with three claws round its emitter, which moves moving structures by hand.

## What it is for

Setting a moving structure where you want it: lifting it, pushing it, or holding it still in the air.

Table: Its modes

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Grab | Holds the point aimed at before you as you move and look, as a stiff spring that bears its weight |
| Push | Gives it a blow where it is aimed |
| Freeze | Holds it where it stands, in the air too, until it is let go again |

## How to get it

Craft it of a lodestone in the middle, an iron ingot on each of its sides, and an amethyst shard and a redstone dust at two opposite corners. A server may turn the recipe off.

## How to use it

1. Aim at a moving structure and hold the use button to act on it in the tool's mode.
2. Turn the mouse wheel to change the mode, and with Shift its strength, from a tenth to the whole: how hard it holds and pushes.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Nothing moves.
> The tool moves only moving structures. A heavy one needs more strength: turn it up.
