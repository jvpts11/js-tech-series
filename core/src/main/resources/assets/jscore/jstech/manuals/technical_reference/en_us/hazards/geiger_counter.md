# Geiger Counter

## What it is

A radiation meter in a yellow case: its needle and its figures show how much radiation comes at you where you stand, and it clicks faster as it climbs.

## What it is for

Knowing where radiation is before it doses you, and marking on your hazard map the chunks you walk through.

> [!DEFINE] Dose rate
> How fast radiation doses you where you stand, in millisieverts an hour (mSv/h). The ground gives off a little everywhere; near waste or a reactor it climbs.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Keep it in your hotbar or your off hand: it shows on your screen and reads as you walk.
2. Use it to hold it up. RANGE reads ten and then a hundred times higher, for a place where the needle is pinned; SOUND turns its clicks off and on.
3. Where it shows on your screen, and how big, is in the hazard devices' settings.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It shows OFF.
> There is no radiation in this world: the server turned it off, or no mod gives it.

> [!PROBLEM] The needle is pinned at the right.
> The rate is past its scale: press RANGE, and get away.
