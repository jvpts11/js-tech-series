# Saw and Microblocks

## What it is

A blade of iron on a wooden handle, the series' shared saw, and the microblocks it cuts: pieces of any full block, of any mod.

## What it is for

Building in pieces smaller than a block, and dressing cables: several pieces, cables and the parts on them share one block space.

Table: The pieces

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Slab, panel, cover | A half, a quarter and an eighth of a block against one face, and the thicknesses between |
| Hollow | A piece on a face with a hole in its middle, through which a cable still links |
| Post | A bar along an edge of the block, or through its middle |
| Corner | A cube in one corner of the block |
| Facade | A cover joined with a cable: it hides the cable behind it, which keeps working |

Table: Where the saw lies in the crafting grid

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Above the piece | Two halves: a block gives two slabs, a slab two panels, a panel two covers |
| Beside it | Cut across: a piece on a face gives two posts, a post two corners |
| At a corner of it | Hollowed: a piece on a face gives one with a hole |

## How to get it

Craft the saw of a stick and two iron ingots in a row. Each cut wears it a little, and it stays in the grid.

## How to use it

1. Place a piece against the face of a block to put it in the space beyond; place it on the side of a piece already in a space to put it in that same space.
2. A post goes on the edge nearest where you click, or through the middle when you click near the face's middle; a corner in the nearest corner.
3. Two pieces of one shape and one block, side by side in the grid, join into one as thick as both; two slabs make the block again.
4. Join a cover with a piece of any cable to make a facade, and place it on a cable's face.
5. Break a piece to take only it, with its drop; the others stay.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A piece is not placed.
> Something already fills that place, it would overlap a piece there or reach into a cable, or someone stands in it. On a cable, use a cover, a panel or a hollow piece.

> [!PROBLEM] A cable stops linking on a face.
> A whole cover closes the face; use a hollow one or a facade there.
