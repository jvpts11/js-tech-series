# Vehicles

## What it is

Every vehicle of the mods and data packs you have: each placed from a Vehicle item that names its kind, and made of parts, each a Vehicle Part item while it is out of one.

## What it is for

Travelling and hauling: its seats carry players and robots, and whoever sits in a driving seat drives it.

## How to get it

As the mod or the data pack that brings the vehicle says. In creative, the Tools & Utilities tab holds every kind and every part.

## How to use it

1. Use the item on the ground to place the vehicle there, facing the way you look: it is yours, and its key is cut for you when its kind has keys.
2. Use the vehicle to get in; in a driving seat, the walking keys drive it, as it burns its fuel or draws on its battery.
3. Use a part on the vehicle near its empty place of that kind to fit it; sneak with a wrench to take one out, or hold its material in the other hand to mend a worn one.
4. Use a dye on it to paint it, and its key with the sneak key held to lock or unlock it.
5. Sneak with an empty hand near its hitch to hitch a trailer to it, or to let it go.
6. Hit it to break it back into its item, which keeps its parts, its fuel and its load, and places it again as it was.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It does not move.
> Its fuel or its battery is empty, or a part it needs is missing or worn out.

> [!PROBLEM] It cannot be changed or broken.
> It is someone else's: only its owner and whom they trust may set it up.
