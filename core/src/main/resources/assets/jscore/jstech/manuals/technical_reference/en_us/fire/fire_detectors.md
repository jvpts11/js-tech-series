# Fire Detectors

## What it is

Detectors hung under a ceiling: a smoke detector sees the smoke that rises to it, a heat detector feels the air reach 57 °C. Their lamp lights while they call.

## What it is for

Raising the alarm, or setting off anything you wire to them, when a fire starts.

> [!DEFINE] Obscuration
> How much of the light the smoke takes away, in percent. A smoke detector calls at ten.

## How to get it

In creative, take them from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need them give them their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place one under a ceiling above what might burn: smoke rises, so a fire above it is not seen.
2. Each gives redstone on every side: full while it calls, or, chosen with a signal probe, the smoke or the temperature it reads.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] It never calls.
> The fire is above it or too far to one side: hang it higher, over the fire.
