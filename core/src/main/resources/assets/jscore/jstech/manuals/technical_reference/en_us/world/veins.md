# Veins

## What it is

Ores do not lie scattered through the rock: they lie in veins. A vein is a body of ore dense at its middle and thinning to nothing at its edge, so one place holds nothing and the next holds hundreds of blocks of ore.

> [!DEFINE] Zone
> A square of the world three chunks a side. A zone holds one vein at most, and most hold none.

## What it is for

Telling where to dig. A vein of iron near the surface feeds a furnace for a long time; a vein of gold deep down is worth a long tunnel.

## How to get it

Every world has them, and they lie where the world's seed says, the same on every server with that seed. These are the veins of the series; a modpack or a mod may change them and add others.

Table: The veins of the series

| Vein | Its middle lies | Found | Across, up and down |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | from y -48 to 96 | in the Overworld, about one zone in six | 20 blocks, 6 |
| Copper | from y -16 to 112 | in the Overworld, about one zone in eight | 22 blocks, 7 |
| Gold | from y -64 to 32 | in the Overworld, about one zone in twenty | 16 blocks, 5 |
| Gold of the Nether | from y 10 to 117 | in the Nether, about one zone in five | 18 blocks, 6 |
| Tin | from y -16 to 80 | in the Overworld, about one zone in twelve, once a mod uses tin | 18 blocks, 6 |

## How to use it

1. Look where ore shows on a cave's wall or a cliff: a vein is far larger than what shows, so dig round it.
2. Follow it the way its ore grows thicker: that way lies its middle.
3. Follow it from rock to rock: the same vein lays iron ore in the stone and deepslate iron ore in the deepslate.
4. With a mod's prospecting instrument, learn which way the nearest veins lie and how strongly each shows; standing in one, what it holds and about how much.
5. When a vein is mined out, look a few chunks on: the next is at least a zone away.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] No ore anywhere, for chunks on end.
> Most zones hold no vein. Go on a few chunks, or look deeper or higher: each kind lies between its heights.

> [!PROBLEM] The game's iron is gone from where it used to lie.
> While ores lie in veins, the game's scattered iron, copper and gold are not laid. A server turns veins off in `jstech-balance.toml` (`veins.enabled`); only chunks made afterwards change.

> [!PROBLEM] A vein holds its ore in one rock and not in another.
> An ore lies only in a rock a mod has it in: a block of a vein whose material has no ore in that rock is left as it was.
