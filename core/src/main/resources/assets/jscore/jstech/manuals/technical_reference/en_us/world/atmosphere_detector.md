# Atmosphere Detector

## What it is

A gauge for the air: it reads the air round it, a sealed room's or the open air's, and shows what it is made of, its pressure and the room it stands in.

## What it is for

Knowing whether a room can be breathed before you take your helmet off, and finding where a room leaks its air.

> [!DEFINE] Sealed room
> A space walled in on every side by blocks that hold air. It keeps its own air: filled with oxygen it can be breathed on the Moon; a hole in it lets its air out.

> [!DEFINE] Kilopascal
> The unit of pressure on the display, kPa. The air at sea level presses at about 101 kPa; a breath needs oxygen at more than 16 kPa of it.

## How to get it

In creative, take it from the J's Tech tab; the mods that need it give it their recipes.

## How to use it

1. Place it in the room, or in its wall: a detector in a wall reads the room beside it.
2. Use it to open its screen. THE AIR lists each gas and its share, the pressure, and whether it is falling or rising.
3. THE ROOM draws the room from above, its leaks marked, with its size and how many leaks it has; OPEN AIR when no room holds it.
4. Press SHOW IN THE WORLD to see the room outlined round you, its leaks lit, while you walk it; press it again to stop.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The HYPOXIA lamp is lit.
> There is too little oxygen to breathe: fill the room with oxygen, or keep your helmet on.

> [!PROBLEM] The LEAKING lamp is lit.
> The room loses its air somewhere: show it in the world and close the leaks lit, a missing block, an open door or a pane.

> [!PROBLEM] The VACUUM lamp is lit.
> There is no air at all: the room is open to space, or was never filled.
