# Energy (FE)

## What it is

Energy is what machines and computers run on. It is counted in FE, the same unit most machine mods use, so a generator of another mod can run these machines and the other way round.

## What it is for

A generator makes it, a cable carries it, a machine spends it while it works.

> [!DEFINE] Tick
> The game's beat: 20 ticks make a second. 40 FE a tick is 800 FE a second.

> [!DEFINE] Buffer
> The energy a machine can store, like a small battery inside it. It keeps a machine working for a while after its power stops.

## How to get it

Make it with a generator, such as J's Industrial's Coal Generator.

## How to use it

1. Place a generator and give it fuel.
2. Put machines against it, or lay energy cable from it to them.
3. Watch a machine's energy bar: it fills while energy comes in.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A machine works in bursts.
> It spends more a tick than its generators make. Add a generator.

> [!PROBLEM] The generator burns fuel but nothing gets energy.
> Nothing touches it and no cable runs from it. Join a machine to it.
