# Tools and items

Items that keep a state and do more than one thing: tools with modes, tools that mine or build over an area, tools on
energy, fuel or air, and items that answer a key without being held.

## What exists today

- **Items that hold things**, energy among them, keep their contents as data components ([Content](../CONTENT.md)).
- **What is worn** is found wherever it is worn ([Entities](../ENTITIES.md)).

## To build

The kit gathers what Mekanism's Meka-Tool and Atomic Disassembler, Tinkers' Construct, Mining Gadgets, Building
Gadgets, FTB Ultimine and the Construction Wand do, in one place, for any mod.

### Modes and actions

- **Tool modes**, switched by a key, by the mouse wheel or by a radial menu, and shown on the HUD.
- **Actions of items not held**: an item in the inventory or in a worn slot answers a key (turning a jetpack on,
  switching a magnet).
- **Charging and cooldowns**: hold to charge (a railgun of J's Warfare, a drill that heats up) and wait between uses.

### Mining and building

- **Mining over an area** (3 by 3, 5 by 5, with depth) and **along a vein** (the ore that touches), within limits,
  respecting claimed land and the hardness of blocks ([The platform](platform.md#permissions-and-protection)).
- **A preview in the world**: the outline of the area about to be mined, and ghosts of what a building tool is about to
  place.
- **Building tools**: many blocks placed along a line or a plane, as the Construction Wand does; **a region copied and
  pasted**, as Building Gadgets does; the cost taken from the inventory, or from the network with J's Computers.

### Energy and modules

- **Tools on energy, fuel or compressed air**: a chainsaw on petrol, J's Industrial's pneumatic tools.
- **Modules in tools**, through the same kit as worn modules ([Entities and vehicles](entities.md)), as the Meka-Tool's
  modules (reach, vein, silk touch on or off).

### Showing the state

- **Several bars on an item's icon**: durability, energy, fluid, and the mode it is in.
- **Durability can come from a property of the material**, when a mod wants it; it is the mod's choice, since the
  material catalogue keeps only real data, never game rules ([Materials](materials.md)).
