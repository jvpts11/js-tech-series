# Manuals

The player learns the mod in a **manual** inside the game: what each block and item is, what it is for, how it is
obtained, how it is used and what can go wrong. J's Core draws the book, numbers it, builds the index and the search,
and opens it at an item's page; J's Computers writes the pages of its chapter.

## The series' manuals

| Manual | What it has | How it looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical Reference (J's Core) | every chapter of the mods installed | a navy blue binder, with a tab per chapter |
| Guide to Operations (J's Computers) | J's Computers' chapter | the beige binder of the first computers' manuals, with a cyan stripe |
| Plant Drawings (J's Industrial) | J's Industrial's chapter | a binder of plans: the list, and each machine in three views |

## J's Computers' chapter

The sections Getting Started, Hardware, Computers, Peripherals, Storage, Network, Operations, Autocrafting and Systems,
in the voice of a guide, with each entry's item drawn under its title, links in the text to the other entries, and
pictures of the machines and of the firmware, live. Entries are written once and show in every manual that has the
chapter.

## Using a manual

- The Technical Reference is given to each player once, the first time they join a world.
- A manual opens on its cover the first time, and afterwards on the page where it was closed. Arrows, Page Up and Page
  Down and the wheel turn the pages; Home goes to the index; the magnifying glass searches the index as you type.
- **The manual key** (M, configurable): held a moment over an item, in an inventory or in hand, it opens the manual at
  the item's page, with a bar that fills in the style of the manual about to open. It opens the item's mod's manual
  before the Technical Reference. The tooltip of an item with a page says so.
- On a computer, every system's help reads the same manuals beside its commands
  ([Operating systems](operating-systems.md)).
