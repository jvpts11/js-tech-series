# Abandoned buildings

Twenty kinds of building lie abandoned across the Overworld, and you find each of one era: the Vintage near the world's spawn, the Advanced the farther you go. A house with an old computer in its attic, a dump of broken machines, a shop, a school, a library, a bank with a Mainframe in its vault, a factory of the chain's machines, a datacenter, a supercomputing centre.

About one in five still works: its machines whole, their systems installed, ready to come up as soon as you give them energy. The rest are ruins, their computers open with half their parts in their seats, and you find their loot in chests and hardware boxes.

Every building holds a way to a graphical system in its main chest: a system with its own desktop, or in the Vintage UNIX System V and CDE for it.

> [!DEFINE] Hardware box
> A carton found in the buildings, printed with its era's machine: a computer's parts, or a graphics card. It opens once and stays open.
