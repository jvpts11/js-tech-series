# Notices and the Action Center

A notice is a message that the desktop or a program sends you. Frames has three kinds of notice, and each one asks something different of you. A dialog waits for your answer. A balloon says one thing and goes away by itself. A toast is the Frames 10 form of the balloon.

Table: The kinds of notice

| Kind | What it does | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dialog | Waits until you answer it: an error, a question, or the Open with chooser. | In the middle of the screen, over everything else. |
| Balloon | Says one thing, then goes away by itself after 9 seconds. | Over the status area of the taskbar, on Frames 95, Frames XP, Frames 7 and Frames 11. |
| Toast | The Frames 10 form of the balloon. | In a corner of the screen, on Frames 10 only. |

## Dialogs

An error dialog has the title "Error". A question offers the buttons "OK", "Yes" and "No", and you choose one of them. A click beside a dialog makes the computer beep, and the dialog stays on the screen. The Escape key closes a dialog.

## Balloons

A balloon plays the notify sound when it appears, unless Quiet hours is on. These are some of the balloons you may see:

Table: Some balloons

| Title | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Low on memory | The memory has passed nine tenths. It names the three programs that hold the most, and what each holds. A program that does not fit after that brings the system down ([](jsc:frames_memory)). |
| Not enough video memory | A program needs more video memory than the graphics card has free. |
| Cannot open | No program on this computer opens the item you chose. |

## The history of notices

Each notice goes into a list, with the newest first. The list keeps up to 12 notices. It lasts while the desktop is open, and the list clears when the desktop closes.

## The Action Center, on Frames 10

The Action Center is a dark pane down the right edge of the screen. Open it with the Action Center button at the far right of the taskbar, and close it with the same button. Opening it marks the notices as read.

The list is headed "Notifications", with "Clear all" beside it, and each notice opens its program when you click it. If there are no notices, the pane says "No new notifications". Four quick actions sit along its foot.

Table: The quick actions

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| All settings | Closes the pane, and opens Settings on its first page, Personalize. |
| Network | Opens the Network Manager. If it cannot open, Settings opens instead. |
| Quiet hours | Turns Quiet hours on or off. The button is lit while it is on. |
| Sound | Opens Settings on the Sound page. |

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours keeps notices from popping up. While it is on, a notice goes to the list only: no balloon appears, and no notify sound plays. The switch does not mute dialogs, so an error or a question still appears.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] No notice appears on the screen.
> On Frames 10, Quiet hours may be on. Open the Action Center: a lit Quiet hours button means it is on. Click it to turn it off.

> [!PROBLEM] The list of notices is empty after you open the desktop again.
> The list lasts only while the desktop is open, so it clears when the desktop closes.

> [!DEFINE] dialog
> A box in the middle of the screen that waits until you answer it.

> [!DEFINE] balloon
> A small box over the status area of the taskbar, which goes away by itself after 9 seconds.

> [!DEFINE] toast
> The Frames 10 form of the balloon, shown in a corner of the screen.

> [!DEFINE] Quiet hours
> A switch that keeps notices from popping up. The notices still go to the list.
