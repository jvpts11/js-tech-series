# Personalising: themes, wallpapers and colours

Personalize is the page where you change how the desktop looks. You choose a wallpaper and the clock format. On the richer editions you also choose an accent and a theme. Frames 10 and Frames 11 add a light or dark look, and Frames 11 lets you align the taskbar.

The [Effects](jsc:effects) entry sits at the top of this page. It leads to the effects of your edition.

Table: What Personalize offers, by edition

| Edition | The rows of this page |
| --- | --- |
| Frames 95 | The wallpaper and the clock. |
| Frames XP | The wallpaper, the accent, the themes and the clock. |
| Frames 7 | The wallpaper, the accent, the themes and the clock. |
| Frames 10 | The wallpaper, the accent, the themes, the light or dark look and the clock. |
| Frames 11 | The wallpaper, the accent, the themes, the light or dark look, the taskbar alignment and the clock. |

## Wallpapers

The grid shows the desktop's own wallpaper first, and then every wallpaper that you can choose. Each edition starts with its own.

Table: The wallpapers of the Frames editions

| Wallpaper | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Frames 95 | A classic teal background. |
| Frames XP | A green hill under a blue sky. |
| Frames 7 | A night blue, banded with falling light. |
| Frames 10 | A dark room, with a window of four panes lit. |
| Frames 11 | The blue bloom, with a dark picture for the dark look. |
| Other desktops | The backdrops of KDE, GNOME, Cinnamon and FreeBSD. |

The backdrop of the Common Desktop Environment (CDE) cannot be chosen on a Frames.

## Accents and themes

The accent is the highlight colour of your desktop. You choose one of six accents: blue, green, orange, violet, pink or amber. A theme sets the accent and the wallpaper for you. Frames has three themes:

Table: The themes

| Theme | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| system | No change to the accent; the desktop keeps its own wallpaper. |
| ocean | The green accent, and the Frames XP wallpaper, the green hill. |
| slate | The violet accent, and the Frames 11 wallpaper, the blue bloom. |

The theme you chose appears as the highlighted button.

## Light and dark

Frames 10 and Frames 11 have a Light and a Dark look. The other editions have no such row: Frames 7 and Frames XP have no dark look.

## Taskbar alignment, on Frames 11

Frames 11 offers Center and Left for the taskbar. Center puts the Start button and the program icons together in the middle, and it is the default. Left moves them to the left edge.

## The clock

The clock shows 24-hour time by default. Choose 12-hour to change it on any edition.

1. Open Settings, and open the Personalize page.
2. Click a wallpaper in the grid.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The wallpaper you want is not in the grid.
> Only the wallpapers the game offers appear there.

> [!PROBLEM] Frames 95 has no accent or theme row.
> The classic look of Frames 95 has no accent or theme rows. Change the wallpaper and the clock instead.

> [!PROBLEM] Frames 7 or Frames XP has no light or dark look.
> Use Frames 10 or Frames 11 for a light or dark look.
