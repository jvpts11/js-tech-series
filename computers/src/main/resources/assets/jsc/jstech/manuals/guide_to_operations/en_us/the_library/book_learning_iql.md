# Learning IQL

Learning IQL teaches the network's query language by example: a query, its answer, and why it works. You ask the network questions in IQL, and the network answers them by running Operations. Start with [What IQL and Operations are](jsc:what_iql_is) if you are new.

Its chapters are Getting started, The language (every verb and clause, and every error), The engines (the three engines and how to switch them), The tools, and Recipes, which are ready queries.

No system's help opens this book first. You reach it from [The library](jsc:library), or you take it from the creative tab.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You do not have this book.
> Take it from the creative tab J's Computers, among the programs, beside the Guide.
