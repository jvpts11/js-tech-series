# Frames Resource Kit

The Frames Resource Kit is the reference for MC-DOS and the Frames editions, from Frames 95 to Frames 11. It is written the way a 1990s technical kit was: thorough and exact, with tables of settings and commands. You keep it open while you work on a Midsoft system.

Its chapters are The Midsoft systems, MC-DOS, The desktop, The programs of Frames, Each edition, and The Command Prompt. If you have not chosen a system yet, start with [Which Frames goes on which machine](jsc:frames_which_machine).

The help of MC-DOS and of every Frames opens this book first.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You do not have this book.
> Take it from the creative tab J's Computers, among the programs, beside the Guide.
