# Linux by Hand

Linux by Hand is a lab notebook. It takes you through one step at a time: what to type, what the machine answers, and what each step does and why. It covers the two systems you install by hand, Arch Linux from the live medium and Gentoo from source.

Its chapters are Before you begin, The disks, Arch, Gentoo, When the reboot refuses, and the four UNIX chapters it shares with the other books.

Its help opens first for Arch Linux and for Gentoo. Start with [Installing by hand](jsc:installing_by_hand).

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You do not have this book.
> Take it from the creative tab J's Computers, among the programs, beside the Guide.
