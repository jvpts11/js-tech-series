# Sigma and Sigma#

This chapter is the reference of Sigma and Sigma#, the languages the programs of your computers are written in. Each page after this one is a namespace of the library: every type and call in it, what each call gives back, what it costs in credits beyond the instruction that makes it, and the version of the language it came in.

You can read it in this binder or on any of your computers, through the system's help: info sigma on a Linux, the Help of a desktop, or the manuals of the terminal. The pages are written from the library itself, so they say what your machines run.

The Book of Sigma teaches the languages: the files a program is made of and the forms it takes ([](jsc:sigma_first_program), [](jsc:sigma_entry_points)), every error the compiler gives ([](jsc:sigma_reading_an_error)), the costs of running ([](jsc:call_costs)), the debugger ([](jsc:sigma_debugging)) and what each version brought ([](jsc:sigma_version_three)).
