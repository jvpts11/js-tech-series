# Windows: move, resize, minimise, maximise, snap and Task View

A window is the box that holds one open program. Its title bar runs along the top, with the program's name and three boxes. The corner at the bottom right is the resize grip.

Table: The boxes of a window

| Box | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Minimise box | The window goes down to its button on the taskbar. Click that button to bring the window back. |
| Maximise or restore box | A maximised window fills the work area. The same box returns the window to its old size. |
| Close box | The window closes. The program may ask you first. |

## Moving and resizing

1. Drag the title bar to move the window.
2. Drag the resize grip to change the size of the window.

The window stays on the screen: its title bar cannot be dragged off the desktop. A program sets the smallest size its windows can take, so the window stops shrinking at that size.

## Snap, on Frames 11

On Frames 11, you can snap a window by letting it go at an edge of the screen. Let go with the mouse pointer at the top edge of the work area, and the window maximises. Let go at the left edge, and it takes the left half of the work area. Let go at the right edge, and it takes the right half.

Windows do not snap on Frames 95, Frames XP, Frames 7 or Frames 10.

## Task View, on Frames 10

On Frames 10, the Task View button on the taskbar shows every open window at once. The desktop dims, and each window becomes a card: its program's icon and its title, over a live picture of the window.

A click on a card brings that window to the front. A click anywhere else, or on the Task View button again, puts the view away.

## Showing the desktop

Frames 7 has a narrow corner at the far right of the taskbar. Click it to show the desktop. Every edition has the same row in the taskbar's menu, Show the Desktop, and both minimise every window.

Cascade Windows, in the same menu, arranges the windows in a cascade.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A window will not snap to the top edge of the screen.
> Snap works on Frames 11 only. On the other editions, use the maximise box.

> [!PROBLEM] You look for the Task View button, and it is not on the taskbar.
> Only Frames 10 has the Task View button. Frames 95, Frames XP, Frames 7 and Frames 11 have none.

> [!DEFINE] title bar
> The strip along the top of a window, with its name and its three boxes.

> [!DEFINE] resize grip
> The small corner at the bottom right of a window, which you drag to change its size.

> [!DEFINE] snap
> To let a window go at a screen edge, so that it fills half or all of the work area.

> [!DEFINE] work area
> The desktop minus the taskbar: the part of the screen a maximised window fills.
