# Frames Resource Kit

This kit is the reference for Midsoft's systems: MC-DOS and every edition of Frames, from 95 to 11. It tells you which one goes on which machine, how to install it, and everything on its desktop and at its prompt.

Each command has a page of its own, the same page HELP and /? show at the prompt.

The magnifier on the top edge finds anything by name, and the blocks down the edge of the right page each open a chapter.
