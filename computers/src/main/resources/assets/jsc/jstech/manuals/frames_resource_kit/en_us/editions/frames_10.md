# Frames 10

Frames 10 is the fourth edition. You need a Standard computer or newer, and it installs from a USB stick that goes in the [Dock Station](jsc:dock_station). Its footprint is 16,384 MB, which is 64 items on a Standard disk.

Its look is Metro: a dark taskbar with a search box and a task view beside the Start button. Start is a list with live tiles, and the Action Center sits at the right edge of the screen.

> [!DEFINE] Live tile
> A square in the Start list that can show a changing value.

> [!DEFINE] Task view
> The button that shows every open window.

> [!DEFINE] Action Center
> The panel at the right edge of the screen that gathers the notices.

The file program is called File Explorer. Help is called Get Help, and its window is called Get Help too.

## Installing Frames 10

1. Put the Frames 10 install stick in the Dock Station ([](jsc:dock_station)).
2. Start the computer from the stick. If the computer does not start from it, open the Boot Menu ([](jsc:boot_menu)).
3. Choose a disk on the disk page.
4. Wait while the installer goes through "Copying Frames files", "Getting files ready for installation", "Installing features", "Installing updates" and "Finishing up".
5. Answer the question "Who's going to use this PC?".
6. Wait until the installer finishes.

The installer is a white window with a thin blue border over a dark ground. When you reach the set-up that follows, it fills the screen in blue.

Its Task Manager is the modern form, with the pages Processes, Performance, Services, Storage, Network and GPU. The GPU page is described in [Frames 11](jsc:frames_11).

Its Device Manager is flat: the modern form that Frames 11 kept.

Personalize > Effects holds the visual effects. Its note reads "How Frames looks and moves. A weak graphics card turns both off by itself." The page has two switches: "Transparency effects" and "Play animations in Frames".

On a new computer, Frames 10 says "Hi.", the first edition of the family to do so. It plays "Frames 10 chimes" when it starts.

Its splash screen is black, with a window of four panes in blue, and a ring of dots chases round below it. It has no error sound of its own: when something goes wrong it plays the Frames 11 error sound, "Frames 11 error".

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The installer says "Frames 10 needs Standard era hardware or newer; this machine is Legacy era."
> A Legacy computer cannot run Frames 10. Frames XP runs on it ([](jsc:frames_xp)).

> [!PROBLEM] The installer says "Frames 10 needs Standard era hardware or newer; this machine is Transition era."
> A Transition computer cannot run Frames 10. Frames 7 runs on it ([](jsc:frames_7)).
