# The Fab, from the ore to a computer

The Fab is everything that turns what you dig into the parts of a computer: your Electronics Workbench, and the machines of the chain, each a multiblock you build first ([](jsc:building_chain_machines)). You do not need all of it at once. A bench makes the parts of its era by hand, and each machine you add makes more of them, or makes what a bench cannot.

> [!DEFINE] Fab
> A factory that makes chips and the parts round them, as the chip makers call their plants.

## The order you use it in

1. Crush, grind and smelt the ore, then roll and draw the metal ([](jsc:ore_and_metal_machines)).
2. Run the chemistry: the liquids and gases the later machines ask for ([](jsc:chemistry_machines)).
3. Grow the silicon and saw it into wafers ([](jsc:silicon_machines)).
4. Make the chips, a layer at a time: a Lithography Station exposes each layer ([](jsc:lithography_stations)), and the chip factory's machines do the rest ([](jsc:chip_factory_machines)).
5. Make the boards, the parts beside the chips, the glass and the screens ([](jsc:board_machines)).
6. Assemble the parts at your Electronics Workbench ([](jsc:electronics_workbench)), or let an Electronics Assembly Line assemble them on its own ([](jsc:electronics_assembly_lines)).
7. Put whole computers together and write what they run with the Prebuilder and the Preinstaller ([](jsc:computer_making_machines)).

The chain's own parts, made on the way, are listed with the era each first appears in ([](jsc:chain_parts)). Books are printed off the chain, at the Large Scale Printer ([](jsc:large_scale_printer)).

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A machine you built does not start.
> It is a multiblock: check that every block of its shape is in place, and that it gets its energy ([](jsc:building_chain_machines)).

> [!PROBLEM] A recipe asks for something no machine of yours makes.
> It comes from an earlier step of the chain. Follow the steps above back to the machine that makes it.
