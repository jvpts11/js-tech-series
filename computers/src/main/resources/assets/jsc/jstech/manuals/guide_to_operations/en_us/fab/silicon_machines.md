# Silicon

From the silicon of sand to the wafers every chip is made on, these machines stand up tall where you have the room.

The Siemens Reactor turns metallurgical silicon, with hydrogen chloride and hydrogen, into polysilicon under its bell. The Crystal Puller grows a boule from polysilicon and a dopant, and the crystals of gallium arsenide, aluminium gallium indium phosphide, gallium nitride and calcium fluoride, the nitride with the nitrogen of its gas inlet. The Wafer Saw cuts a boule into wafers, polished flat.
