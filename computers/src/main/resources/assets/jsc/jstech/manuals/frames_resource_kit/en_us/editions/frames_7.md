# Frames 7

Frames 7 is the third edition. You need a Transition computer or newer, and it installs from a digital versatile disc (DVD) in the DVD Drive. Its footprint is 16,384 MB, which is 64 items on a Transition disk.

Its look is the glass look. The taskbar is a superbar, and its icons have no titles. Start is a round orb, and a show-desktop button sits at the right edge of the taskbar.

> [!DEFINE] Superbar
> A taskbar made of icons with no titles, as on Frames 7.

The Start menu has two columns, and one of them is a glass panel of places. On Frames 7 the program This PC is called Computer, and the Settings program is called Control Panel.

## Installing Frames 7

1. Put the Frames 7 DVD in the DVD Drive, and start the computer from it. If the computer does not start from it, open the Boot Menu ([](jsc:boot_menu)).
2. Choose a disk on the disk page.
3. Wait while the installer goes through "Copying Frames files", "Expanding Frames files", "Installing features", "Installing updates" and "Completing installation".
4. Type a name for the computer on the page that says "Choose a name for your computer to distinguish it on the network."
5. Wait until the installer finishes.

Frames 7 calls its help program Help and Support. Its window is called "Frames Help and Support" and has a "Search Help" box.

Its Task Manager has the four tabs of Frames XP, and its Device Manager is the console of Frames XP, in glass.

Its Performance Options page is reached from System, as on Frames XP, with the same visual effects and one more check: "Enable transparent glass". Under the heading "Ease of Access: make the computer easier to see" is a switch, "Turn off all unnecessary animations (when possible)".

On a new computer, Frames 7 shows its own Welcome, not "Hi.". It plays "Frames 7 chimes" when it starts. Its error sound is Frames XP's, "Frames XP error".

Its splash screen is black: four coloured lights sweep in and close into a waving flag, with the name of the system under it.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The installer says "Frames 7 needs Transition era hardware or newer; this machine is Legacy era."
> A Legacy computer cannot run Frames 7. Frames XP runs on it ([](jsc:frames_xp)).
