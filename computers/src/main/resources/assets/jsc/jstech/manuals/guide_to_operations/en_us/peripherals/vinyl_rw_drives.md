# The Vinyl RW Drive

The Vinyl RW Drive reads vinyl and cuts it. There is one in each era, and you join it to its computer by a peripheral cable like any device. Soundfoundry drives it: the VNL button on its Legacy and Transition looks, and its Vinyl page on the Standard and the Advanced.

To record, lay a music disc on its platter, the game's or another mod's, and press Record. The drive plays the song aloud in the room, and Soundfoundry records it, in the song's own time, into an audio file in Music/Vinyl. The recording is kept when the song ends; stopped before, it is lost.

To cut, lay a Blank Vinyl Record on the platter, pick a recording and press Cut. The drive cuts it, again in the song's time, and the cut is the original disc itself: music discs are rare, and they are the one thing a copy may make. Only a song that came from a disc goes onto a vinyl. Blank Vinyl Records are made at the Electronics Workbench, of 2 PVC sheets and 1 paper.

> [!DEFINE] Platter
> The turntable of the drive, where the record lies while it plays or is cut.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Put a music disc on the drive to record it.
> Right-click the drive with a music disc to lay it on the platter.

> [!PROBLEM] Put a blank vinyl in the drive to cut onto it.
> Right-click the drive with a Blank Vinyl Record.

> [!PROBLEM] Only a song that came from a disc goes onto a vinyl.
> Cut a recording the drive made from a disc, or a copy of one.

> [!PROBLEM] The recording could not be kept on the computer's disk.
> Make room on the computer's disk, then record the song again.
