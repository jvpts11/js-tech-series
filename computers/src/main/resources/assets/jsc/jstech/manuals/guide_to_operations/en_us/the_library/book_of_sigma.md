# The Book of Sigma

The Book of Sigma is the classic language book: a definition, then a program that shows it, and the programs are the star. It teaches Sigma and Sigma#, the two languages the computers are programmed in, with their library and their tools.

Its chapters are Getting started, The language, The library (every class and every member), Programs running, Errors, Sharing and Examples.

No system's help opens this book first. You reach it from [The library](jsc:library), or you take it from the creative tab.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You do not have this book.
> Take it from the creative tab J's Computers, among the programs, beside the Guide.
