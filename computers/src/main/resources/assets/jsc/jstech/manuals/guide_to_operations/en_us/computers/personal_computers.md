# Personal Computers

The Personal Computer (PC) is your own computer, the one you sit at. You install a system on it and run programs, and from it you reach the network: you store and take items, watch the machines work, and ask the network to craft.

Inside, it takes one motherboard, one processor (CPU), four memory modules (random-access memory, or RAM), four graphics cards (graphics processing units, or GPUs), two disks and one power supply.

Its storage is the space of its own disks, counted in storage slots: up to 18 slots.

Every era has its own Personal Computer. From the Standard on, you can choose a plain case, a High Performance case or an Aesthetic case. The Vintage, Legacy and Transition eras have one case each.

Some cards serve the Workshop program, which gives you a crafting table, a furnace, an enchanting table and an anvil in one window. Each card works on a Personal Computer from the Legacy on, and each draws 15 W of power.

Table: The Workshop cards

| Card | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Crafting Table Card | A crafting table, on a card |
| Furnace Card | A furnace that needs no fuel, on a card |
| Enchanting Card | An enchanting table with every bookshelf, on a card |
| Anvil Card | An anvil that never wears, on a card |

The Workshop keeps what you put in its window in the computer: the crafting grid, the furnace's input and output, the item to enchant and the items on the anvil. It is lost when the computer breaks.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The assembly screen says "Not a computer yet".
> A computer needs a motherboard, a processor, memory, a disk and a power supply.

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says the processor's socket does not fit the board's socket.
> Choose a processor with the socket the motherboard takes ([](jsc:motherboards)).

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says there are too many PCI Express cards for the board.
> Take a card out, or use a motherboard with more PCI Express slots.

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says the power supply is insufficient.
> Use a power supply that gives more watts than the parts draw ([](jsc:power_supplies)).

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says the board does not take that kind of memory.
> Use memory of a kind the board takes ([](jsc:memory)).
