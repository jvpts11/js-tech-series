# Achievements

An achievement is a step the game records for you the first time you do something worth noting, such as building a working computer or booting your first system. You earn each one once, and the game lists them in six tabs: Fab, Hardware, Operating Systems, Networks & Operations, Sigma and Teracoin.

Table: The tabs

| Tab | What it rewards |
| --- | --- |
| Hardware | Building computers, racks, datacenters and clusters. |
| Operating Systems | Installing and booting systems, and the programs you run on them. |
| Networks & Operations | Bringing a network to life, and running its Operations, the query language IQL, and autocrafting. |
| Sigma | Writing, compiling and running Sigma and Sigma# programs. |
| Fab | The chain from the ore to the chip: its machines, its masks and its stations. |
| Teracoin | Wallets, mining, the pools and the 3D printers. |

Each tab starts with a root, its first achievement, and the others hang from it. Every achievement has a kind. A task is a plain step, a goal is a larger milestone, a challenge asks for several separate things, and a secret stays hidden in its tab until you earn it. Once you earn a secret, its description shows too.

The game credits you for what you do yourself: when you do the thing, the achievement is yours. A machine that works on its own, such as a computer that finishes its power-on self-test, or a job that runs at a set time, credits its operator instead.

If you were offline when it happened, the achievement waits for you and appears at your next login. The game keeps up to 128 waiting achievements for each player.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] An achievement you earned did not appear.
> Check who did the action. Your own actions earn it for you, and a machine's achievement goes to its operator. If you were offline, it appears at your next login.

> [!PROBLEM] A machine's achievement went to another player.
> The operator is the last player who placed or used that machine. Use the machine yourself, and its later achievements are yours.
