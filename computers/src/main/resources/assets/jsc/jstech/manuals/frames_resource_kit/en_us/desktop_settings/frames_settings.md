# Settings and the Control Panel

Settings is where you change how your Frames works: its look, its sound, its network and its disks. It has a left column that lists its pages, and the page you choose appears at the right. On Frames 95, Frames XP, Frames 10 and Frames 11 the program is called Settings. On Frames 7 the same program is called Control Panel.

Frames 7 and Frames 10 open Settings on a home page. On Frames 7 the home page is a list of categories, and on Frames 10 it is a grid of tiles. The other editions open on their first page.

Table: The pages of Settings

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Personalize | Choose the wallpaper and the clock, and on some editions the accent, the theme and the light or dark look. |
| System | Name the computer, read its hardware, and restart into the firmware. |
| Network | See the link of the computer, share folders, and allow or refuse remote programs. |
| Storage | See each disk and its size. The system disk is marked. |
| Display | Set the brightness, and the scale of the screen in percent. |
| Programs | See the installed programs, and uninstall them. |
| Sound | Set the volume, mute the sound, choose the output, and test the sound. |
| Users | Not built yet. The page says "Coming in a future update". |

The page [Personalize](jsc:personalize) is explained on its own page, and so is the Effects page, reached from several of these pages.

## The pages of each edition

Frames 95 lists its eight pages at the left, with no home page. Its [effects page](jsc:effects) is reached from Display.

Frames XP lists the same eight pages. Its Effects page, called Performance Options, is reached from System.

Frames 10 opens on a home page: the heading "Frames Settings", and eight tiles in two rows of four. The tiles are System, Display, Sound, Network, Personalize, Storage, Programs and Users.

Frames 11 lists the same eight pages, with no home page. Its Effects page, called Visual effects, is reached from Personalize.

## The categories of Frames 7

The Control Panel of Frames 7 has seven categories. Each one is a link, with a line of text under its name.

Table: The categories of the Control Panel

| Category | Its line of text | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System and Security | Look after the disks | System |
| User Accounts | The accounts on this computer | Users, which is not built yet |
| Network and Internet | Share folders, allow remote programs | Network |
| Appearance and Personalization | Change the theme | Personalize |
| Hardware and Sound | Adjust the display | Sound, through its title; Display, through its line of text |
| Programs | Uninstall a program | Programs |
| Ease of Access | Optimize visual display | The Effects page, Performance Options |

## Opening a page

1. Open Settings from the Start menu. On Frames 7, open Control Panel.
2. Click a page in the list at the left, or a category or tile on the home page.
3. Change the setting you want on that page.

## The System page

The System page has a box for the name of the computer. It shows "(unnamed)" while the name is empty. Below it are the facts of the hardware: the processor and its speed in megahertz (MHz), the architecture, the memory in megabytes (MB), the graphics memory, the system and the platform.

On Frames 7, the page also shows the [experience rating](jsc:effects), which is a set of scores for the parts. The button "Restart to firmware" restarts the computer into its [firmware](jsc:firmware) setup.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A page says "Coming in a future update".
> The Users page is not built yet, so the game shows that note.

> [!PROBLEM] The button "Restart to firmware" is missing.
> The button shows only when a monitor is linked to the computer. Link a monitor, then open the System page again.

> [!PROBLEM] The Sound page says "No speakers linked".
> Link speakers to the computer's sound output, then look at the page again.

> [!PROBLEM] The Sound page says "No sound hardware".
> The computer has no sound card or board sound. See [Sound cards](jsc:sound_cards).

> [!PROBLEM] The Test button on the Sound page is grey.
> The computer cannot play a sound at the moment, so Test is off.

> [!DEFINE] home page
> The first page of Settings on Frames 7 and Frames 10, which lists the categories or the tiles.

> [!DEFINE] experience rating
> Five scores that rate the processor, the memory, the graphics, the gaming and the disk of a computer.

> [!DEFINE] accent
> The highlight colour of the desktop, used for selections and buttons.

> [!DEFINE] theme
> A named preset that sets the accent and the wallpaper together.
