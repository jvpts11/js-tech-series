# Drives and media

A drive reads removable media: a disk or a disc that you put in, and take out again. You need drives to install systems and programs, and to carry files from one computer to another. Each drive is a peripheral, joined to its computer like any other device ([](jsc:peripheral_cables)).

> [!DEFINE] Linked drive
> A drive joined to the computer, into which you put a disc or a stick, such as a CD Drive or a Dock Station.

Media are named after their formats. CD stands for compact disc, DVD for digital versatile disc, and USB for Universal Serial Bus. A read-only memory (ROM) medium can only be read; a rewritable one (RW or RE) can also be written to.

> [!DEFINE] Format
> The physical kind of medium: floppy, CD, DVD, USB or Blu-ray. It decides which drives read the medium.

Each drive reads only some formats. The table shows what each drive reads.

Table: Drives and what they read

| Drive | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| Floppy Drive | Floppy disks |
| CD Drive | CD-ROMs and CD-RWs |
| DVD Drive | DVD-ROMs and DVD-RWs, and CDs |
| Blu-ray Drive | BD-ROMs and BD-REs, and DVDs and CDs |
| Dock Station | USB flash drives, and storage disks |
| USB port of a case | USB flash drives |

The Dock Station is a drive of its own for storage disks. The USB port on the front of a case takes a flash drive ([](jsc:case_front)).

Table: Media and the drives that read them

| Medium | Format | Writable | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Floppy Disk | Floppy | Yes | Floppy Drive |
| CD-ROM | CD | No | CD Drive, DVD Drive, Blu-ray Drive |
| CD-RW | CD | Yes | CD Drive, DVD Drive, Blu-ray Drive |
| DVD-ROM | DVD | No | DVD Drive, Blu-ray Drive |
| DVD-RW | DVD | Yes | DVD Drive, Blu-ray Drive |
| BD-ROM | Blu-ray | No | Blu-ray Drive |
| BD-RE | Blu-ray | Yes | Blu-ray Drive |
| USB stick | USB | Yes | Dock Station, USB port of a case |

Writable means that the computer can write to the medium as well as read it.

> [!DEFINE] Tray
> The sliding part that a disc sits on. The eject button beside the drive moves it out and back in.

A floppy disk goes into the slot of its Floppy Drive, and a USB flash drive goes into its port with no tray in the way. A disc needs its tray open first.

1. Press the eject button beside the drive to bring the tray out.
2. Click the drive with the disc in your hand, so that it lies on the tray.
3. Press the eject button again, or push the tray in with an empty hand.

A computer sees a drive with its tray out as empty. The tray also stays shut while the computer reads the disc, that is, while it installs from the disc. To take a disc out, sneak-click the drive while its tray is open.

## What a medium holds

Each medium holds what its box says, in bytes. A file takes its own bytes on any medium, so you fit as many files and patterns on a medium as its bytes allow, and its tooltip says how much of it is free. An installer always fits on its medium: the system takes its room on the disk you install it to, not on the medium.

Table: What each medium holds

| Medium | Holds | Era |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floppy Disk | 1.44 MB (1,474,560 bytes) | Vintage |
| CD-ROM and CD-RW | 700 MB (737,280,000 bytes) | Legacy |
| DVD-ROM and DVD-RW | 4.7 GB (4,700,372,992 bytes) | Transition |
| BD-ROM and BD-RE | 25 GB (25,025,314,816 bytes) | Advanced |
| Vaultis Pocket 8G | 8 GB (8,000,000,000 bytes) | Standard, USB 2.0 |
| Vaultis Pocket 16G | 16 GB (16,000,000,000 bytes) | Standard, USB 2.0 |
| Vaultis Pocket 32G | 32 GB (32,000,000,000 bytes) | Standard, USB 3.0 |
| Vaultis Pocket 64G | 64 GB (64,000,000,000 bytes) | Advanced, USB-C |

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] This CD drive cannot read that disc.
> Each medium goes in a drive of its own kind: a CD goes in a CD Drive, and a DVD in a DVD Drive or a Blu-ray Drive.

> [!PROBLEM] The tray is closed - press the eject button to open it.
> Press the eject button beside the drive.

> [!PROBLEM] The drive is reading - wait for it to finish.
> The computer is installing from the disc. Wait until the installation has finished.

> [!PROBLEM] The drive already holds a disc - sneak-click to eject it.
> Sneak-click the drive while its tray is open.
