# Linux Mint on this computer

Linux Mint is a distribution made by Spearmint Linux, and the one that comes up at its desktop: Cinnamon is part of it. It starts behind its own mark with five dots under it, and the Menu button on its panel wears the same mark.

Besides apt at the Terminal, you have two programs of Mint's own in its menu: the Software Manager, which installs programs, and the Update Manager, which keeps them up to date ([](jsc:mint_software_manager), [](jsc:mint_update_manager)).
