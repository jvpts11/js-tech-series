# The chain's parts

On the way from the ore to a computer's parts, the chain makes parts of its own. You assemble each at the Electronics Workbench of its era or a later one ([](jsc:electronics_workbench)), and find it on the tab of the era it first appears in.

Table: The chain's parts

| Parts | From |
| --- | --- |
| Memory and controller chips, their masks and finished wafers | one of each for every era |
| Mask plate, ceramic package, ceramic and tantalum capacitors, ferrite, inductor, transformer | Vintage |
| Tube screen, FR-2 laminate, aluminium bonding wire, floppy film, platter (Vintage) | Vintage |
| Gallium arsenide laser diode, cold cathode lamp, LCD screen, FR-4 laminate, glass fibre, gold bonding wire, platter, blank disc, thermal paste | Legacy |
| AlGaInP laser diode | Transition |
| Gallium nitride laser diode, LED screen, optical fibre | Standard |
| Self-lit panel | Advanced |
| Etched board | one for every era |

The lasers of the CD, DVD and Blu-ray drives come in the eras of their drives, and each screen in the era its kind of monitor first stands on a desk.
