# The Extended CD Drive

The Extended CD Drive is a duplicator tower, two blocks tall: one tray that reads and seven trays that write, as the real towers copied discs by the dozen. It works on its own, with no computer, through its own screen.

Table: The towers

| Tower | Era | Copies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extended CD Drive (Legacy) | Legacy | CDs |
| Extended CD Drive (Transition) | Transition | DVDs and CDs |
| Extended CD Drive (Advanced) | Advanced | Blu-ray discs, DVDs and CDs |

1. Put the disc to copy in the READ tray of its screen.
2. Put a blank disc of the same format, or a larger one, in each writing tray you want a copy in.
3. Press COPY. The tower reads the disc, then writes every blank, its lamps blinking while they write and staying lit once their copy is done.
4. Take the copies out of their trays, or press EJECT.

It copies at the write speed of the medium, and copies what Easy Disc Creator copies ([](jsc:easy_disc_creator)): install discs too, but never a disc that holds the network's storage.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says NOTHING TO READ.
> Put a disc with something on it in the READ tray.

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says HOLDS STORAGE.
> The disc holds the network's storage, and the tower does not copy items.

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says NO BLANKS.
> Put blank discs of a format the tower writes in its writing trays.
