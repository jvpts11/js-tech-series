# Media Player

Every desktop comes with a Media Player, which you do not install: it plays the songs on the computer and its image sequences. Each desktop calls it by its own name.

> [!DEFINE] Media Player
> The player every desktop comes with, for the computer's songs and its image sequences.

> [!DEFINE] Image sequence
> A folder of pictures played one after another in the order of their names: the computers' films.

Table: Its name on each desktop

| Desktop | Called |
| --- | --- |
| Frames 95 and Frames 11 | Media Player |
| Frames XP, 7 and 10 | Frames Media Player |
| KDE Plasma | Dragon Player |
| GNOME | Videos |
| Cinnamon | Celluloid |
| CDE | Audio |
| Quince's systems | QuinceTime Player |

## Songs

It plays the songs in the computer's Music folder, Ogg Vorbis and Wave, through the computer's own player, the one Soundfoundry plays through: what one starts, the other shows. It plays only the songs on the computer; the catalogue and the songs from the network stay Soundfoundry's ([](jsc:soundfoundry)).

A recording needs a sound card. On a computer with none, the player says so and plays nothing, as Soundfoundry does ([](jsc:sound_cards)); its pictures still play.

## Image sequences

Draw the pictures of a film in Paint, or take them with a camera, and keep them in one folder, named so they sort in order: frame01, frame02 and on. The player shows them one after another, 12 a second at first; its Pictures a second option goes from 1 to 20. Each folder of pictures inside your Pictures folder is a sequence the player lists. CDE's Audio shows the picture where it draws a recording's wave.

In Files, a folder's menu has Play, which plays its pictures and its songs, and Add to the player's list, which adds its songs to the computer's list without playing them. A song or a picture also opens in the player from Open with.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The player says the computer has no sound card that can play a song.
> Install a sound card in the computer ([](jsc:sound_cards)).

> [!PROBLEM] A folder of pictures plays in the wrong order.
> The pictures play in the order of their names. Name them so they sort: frame01 before frame10.

> [!PROBLEM] A sequence is not in the player's list.
> Keep its folder inside your Pictures folder, or play it from its folder's menu in Files.
