# Speakers and the subwoofer

A speaker plays a computer's sound somewhere other than the monitor. The Vintage has no speaker; the Legacy and the later eras do. Join a speaker to the computer with a peripheral cable. It takes an audio output of the sound card, or of the board from the Transition on, and each audio output drives two speakers.

> [!DEFINE] Stereo
> A recording with two channels: a left side and a right side.

Two speakers beside a monitor play a stereo recording: the one on the left, as you face the monitor, plays the left side, and the one on the right plays the right side. A speaker on its own, or one that is not beside a monitor, plays both sides.

The speaker's window has a CHANNEL, with one of these settings:

Table: The channel of a speaker

| Channel | What it plays |
| --- | --- |
| Mono, alone | Both sides, as the only speaker of the computer. |
| Mono | Both sides, as one of several speakers. |
| Left, by position | The left side, from its place beside the monitor. |
| Right, by position | The right side, from its place beside the monitor. |
| Off: monitor only | No sound from this speaker: the computer's sound plays only out of the monitor. |
| None | No sound at all, for a speaker that is not linked to the computer. |

The speaker's window also shows what it PLAYS. A Legacy speaker plays "22 kHz, bass and treble cut", so the bass and the treble are cut. A Transition satellite plays "Bass cut: no subwoofer". A Standard or Advanced speaker plays "The whole range".

> [!DEFINE] Satellite
> The small speaker of the Transition set, which plays everything but the bass.

> [!DEFINE] Subwoofer
> A box, with no cable of its own, that plays the bass the satellites leave out.

Stand a subwoofer against one of the computer's linked Transition speakers, on any of that speaker's six faces. Then the Transition speakers of the computer play the bass too. A computer needs only one subwoofer: a second one adds nothing.

Give each speaker a name of up to 32 characters. Programs find a speaker by its name, so no two speakers of one computer can share a name.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The music has no bass.
> Stand a Transition subwoofer against one of the linked Transition speakers.

> [!PROBLEM] The speaker's window says "Not linked".
> Join the speaker to the computer with a peripheral cable ([](jsc:peripheral_cables)).

> [!PROBLEM] Another speaker already has this name.
> Pick another name for this speaker.
