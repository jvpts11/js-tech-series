# The Programmer and the IT Technician

A villager who takes a Programmer's Desk becomes a Programmer, who sells you systems and programs on the media of their era and buys your paper, books and blank media. One who takes a Repair Bench becomes an IT Technician, who sells you parts (boards with their firmware), network cables in packs of eight and monitors, and buys your copper, tin and the parts of older eras.

A villager's level is the era of what it sells: Novice the Vintage, Apprentice the Legacy, Journeyman the Transition, Expert the Standard, Master the Advanced. It keeps the trades of the levels below, and a system can be bought three times before it restocks.

Every kind of village may have a computer shop with both job sites and a Vintage demo computer running MC-DOS. Each job site is two things of the trade on four planks: the floppy disk and the paper for the desk, the solder and the iron ingot for the bench.
