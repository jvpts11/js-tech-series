# Ore and metals

These machines take the ore you dig and make the metal you build with.

The Crusher breaks ore, the block or its raw form, into crushed ore between its toothed rolls, and now and then gives the dust of a rarer metal found with it. The Grinder grinds ingots, crystals and glass into dust. The Induction Furnace melts dusts into ingots and alloys, sand and coke into metallurgical silicon, and coke and calcite into calcium carbide.

The Electrolytic Cell splits water into hydrogen and oxygen and salt into chlorine, refines copper and makes aluminium from alumina. The Rolling Mill rolls an ingot into plate, sheet and foil, and the Wire Drawer draws it into rod and wire, the fine bonding wire too.
