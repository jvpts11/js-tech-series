# Midsoft Lithography on the BSDs and UNIX

Midsoft Lithography is the program of a computer cabled to a Lithography Station from the Legacy on: it shows the station and writes the masks it exposes through ([](jsc:mask_writing)). It installs on FreeBSD, OpenBSD, UNIX System V and Quince as on any system. At a terminal you type litho, and it takes the whole screen: the arrows and [[Tab]] pick the mask, [[W]] writes it on the Mask Blank in the station, and [[F3]] gives the terminal back. On a desktop it opens as a window.
