# 3D printers: TRC into items

A 3D printer turns TRC into items. It prints the real item, a row at a time from the bottom, and puts it out by itself. The Prosa i3 MK3 of the Standard is an open frame that slides each item into its tray; the Rattan Lab X1 of the Advanced is closed, its bed a belt, and prints twice as fast.

Link the printer to your computer with a peripheral cable ([](jsc:peripheral_cables)). The server's settings list what can be printed and the price of each, and that list is all a printer prints.

1. Open the Crypto Wallet and its Print tab.
2. Choose the printer among those linked to the computer.
3. Click the item, and set how many copies.
4. Press Print.

Each item is paid as it starts, from the wallet that sent the job, at once. A wallet short of the price makes the printer wait until it has it; cancel, and nothing more is paid. The TRC paid leave the world for good. A printer draws no energy and uses no filament: its spools are only how it looks.

The printer's window shows the item at work and its layer, the queue with the wallet that pays each job, and the output's three slots. A full output makes it wait.

## Auto print

Auto print, a setting of each wallet, prints one item by itself as the balance grows. You give it the item, the printer and how much the wallet keeps: whenever the balance passes what it keeps by the price of one, one is printed. It runs while the computer holding the wallet's file is on, its window open or not.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The Print tab says no 3D printer is linked to this computer.
> Link one with a peripheral cable to this computer.

> [!PROBLEM] The printer waits for TRC.
> The wallet holds less than the price. Send it TRC, or cancel the job.

> [!PROBLEM] The printer waits for the wallet.
> The computer that sent the job is off, or the wallet's file is gone from it.

> [!PROBLEM] The printer says its output is full.
> Take the items out of its output.
