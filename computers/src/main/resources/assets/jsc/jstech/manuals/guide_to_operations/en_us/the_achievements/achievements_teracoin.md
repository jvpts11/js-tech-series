# Teracoin achievements

The Teracoin tab follows the coin: a wallet, mining, the pools and the 3D printers. A wallet counts for the player who made it, and a miner for its machine's operator.

Table: The Teracoin achievements

| Achievement | What it asks | How it is earned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not Your Keys, Not Your Coins (root) | Make a wallet in the Crypto Wallet. | You make a wallet, on a computer or on a portable. |
| Proof of Work (goal) | Find a block. | One of your miners finds a block, alone or for a pool. |
| Pool Party (task) | Join a mining pool. | One of your miners starts mining for a pool. |
| To the Moon (goal) | Fill every riser of a Mining Computer. | You seat a card on the last empty riser of a rig. |
| Application-Specific (goal) | Mine with an ASIC. | One of your ASICs mines. |
| Pizza Day (task) | Send TRC to another player. | You send TRC to a wallet another player made. |
| HODL (challenge) | Hold 1,000 TRC in one wallet for seven days without sending any. | A wallet you made keeps 1,000 TRC or more for seven game days, nothing leaving it. |
| You Wouldn't Download a Car (task) | Print an item on a 3D printer. | One of your 3D printers puts out an item. |
| The Halvening (secret) | Be there when the reward halves. | You are on the server when a block's reward is half the one before. |

HODL starts over when your wallet sends anything, a 3D printer's payment among it, or drops below 1000 TRC. The game looks at the wallets once a minute.
