# Fab achievements

The Fab tab follows the chain from the ore to the chip, and on to a whole computer. A machine of the chain credits its operator: you, when you are the last player who placed it or used its terminal.

Table: The Fab achievements

| Achievement | What it asks | How it is earned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Some Assembly Required (root) | Make a Vintage Electronics Workbench. | You hold a Vintage Electronics Workbench. |
| Bare Metal (task) | Form your first machine of the chain. | Any machine of the chain stands whole round its terminal. |
| Nine Nines (task) | Grow polysilicon in the Siemens Reactor. | The Siemens Reactor finishes its first work. |
| Pull Request (task) | Pull your first silicon boule. | The Crystal Puller finishes its first work. |
| Wafer-Thin (task) | Cut a boule into wafers. | The Wafer Saw finishes its first work. |
| Tape-Out (task) | Write your first mask. | A Lithography Station writes a mask. |
| First Silicon (goal) | Package your first chip. | The Packaging Line finishes its first work. |
| Bunny Suit (task) | Build a Lithography Station. | A Lithography Station of any era stands whole. |
| Die Shrink (challenge) | Expose a layer in the Lithography Station of every era. | A layer of a wafer of each of the five eras is exposed. |
| 13.5 Nanometres (goal) | Expose a layer with extreme ultraviolet light. | A layer of an Advanced wafer is exposed. |
| Burn-In (task) | Make a screen on the Display Line. | The Display Line finishes its first work. |
| Pick and Place (goal) | Let an Electronics Assembly Line make a part by itself. | An Electronics Assembly Line of any era finishes its first work. |
| Plug and Pray (task) | Build a computer in the Prebuilder. | The Prebuilder finishes a computer. |
| Do Not Turn Off Your Computer (task) | Flash a motherboard's firmware in the Preinstaller. | The Preinstaller finishes a job that writes a board's firmware. |

Die Shrink counts the era of the wafer, not of the station: the ArF station exposes both the Transition's wafers and the Standard's.
