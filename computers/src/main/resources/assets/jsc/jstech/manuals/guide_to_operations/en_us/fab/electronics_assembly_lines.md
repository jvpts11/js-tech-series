# The Electronics Assembly Lines

An Electronics Assembly Line assembles on its own what you assemble by hand at a bench ([](jsc:electronics_workbench)): the parts of its era and of every era before it, by the same recipes, in the same time and at its era's bench's draw.

Each era's line has the machines of its time: axial insertion and wave soldering in the Vintage, DIP insertion and wave soldering in the Legacy, a chip shooter and reflow in the Transition, a gantry and optical inspection in the Standard, two gantries and X-ray inspection in the Advanced. The Feeder Bank and the Wave Solder are the same for the two eras that have them.

Lay the ingredients in through its Item Inlet, in any order, and take the parts from its Item Outlet. With a Crafting Interface against its inlet, the network's autocrafting assembles your parts while you do something else.
