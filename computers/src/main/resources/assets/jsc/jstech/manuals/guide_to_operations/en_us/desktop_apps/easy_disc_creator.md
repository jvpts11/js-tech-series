# Easy Disc Creator

Easy Disc Creator, by Roxa, copies a medium onto a blank one of the same format or a larger one: floppy disks, CDs, DVDs, Blu-ray discs, USB sticks, and vinyl through a Vinyl RW Drive ([](jsc:vinyl_rw_drives)). It comes on a CD and installs on the Frames systems, from Frames 95 on ([](jsc:programs)).

1. Pick the drive to copy from under Copy from, and the drive to record to under Record to.
2. Choose how many copies you want, up to 9 copies.
3. Press Copy. It reads the source, writes the blank and checks the copy, at the write speed of the medium.

With one drive, it reads the source first, then asks for the blank: take the source out, put the blank in and go on. Install discs copy like any other disc. Items never do: it refuses the disks that hold the network's storage and the .dat files.

A vinyl is copied by recording it and cutting it: on a Vinyl RW Drive, it records the disc in the song's own time, asks for a Blank Vinyl Record and cuts the song onto it.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] Easy Disc Creator refuses the disc.
> It holds the network's storage or a .dat file, which never copy.

> [!PROBLEM] The blank is too small.
> Use a blank of the same format or a larger one.
