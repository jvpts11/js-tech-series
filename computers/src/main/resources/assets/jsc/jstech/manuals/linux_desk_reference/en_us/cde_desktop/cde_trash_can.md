# The Trash Can

On CDE the trash is the Trash Can, a control on the Front Panel. Other desktops keep it as an icon on the wallpaper. Its picture shows whether it is empty or full, so you see at a glance whether it holds anything. Click it to open the Trash window.

> [!DEFINE] Put Back
> CDE's word for restoring an item to where it was. Other desktops call this Restore.

> [!DEFINE] Shred
> CDE's word for deleting an item for good. Other desktops call this Delete.

The Trash window has three menus: File, Selected and View. Put Back restores the selected items to where they were. Shred deletes them for good. The window also has Select All, Deselect All, By Name and By Size.

CDE has no Empty Trash button.

Before a delete, CDE asks Confirm File Delete. For one item it asks, for example, "Delete the item in the Home for good?" Its properties show the name, the original location and the size. The original location is Home or Desktop.

## If something goes wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You shredded an item you wanted.
> Shred deletes for good, and the item cannot be put back. Check the selection before you press Shred.

> [!PROBLEM] There is no Empty Trash button.
> Select everything in the Trash window, then press Shred.
