# Sound

How J's Space sounds, and how it doesn't.

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. J's Core's audio framework exists.

## To build

- **Real recordings first**, as in every mod of the series: a sound is found before it is ever made. Here there is an
  advantage: **NASA's recordings are in the public domain**, so the roar of Saturn V and of the Space Shuttle, the
  countdowns and the "liftoff" can be the real ones.
- **There is no sound in vacuum.** Outside a room with air, a player only hears **what touches them**, the tool in hand,
  their own suit, their feet on the ground, muffled, and their own breathing inside the helmet. A starship gliding past
  in silence is more striking than any explosion.
- **Inside a sealed room or a ship**, sound returns as it is in air: machines, pumps, the hum of life support, the hull
  creaking.
- **Air changes sound.** Under Venus' or Titan's thick air sounds carry far and low; in Mars' thin air they are faint
  and short.
- Every sound is listed with its source and licence in the series' asset registry.
