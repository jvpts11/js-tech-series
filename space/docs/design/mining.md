# Mining in space

How J's Space takes what lies beyond the worlds' surfaces: asteroids and small bodies, the depths of ocean worlds, the
air of giant planets, and the places with no world at all. What each place holds is in [Minerals](minerals.md); how it
is brought home is in [Cargo](cargo.md).

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. The containment of exotic matter that the dead stars' harvest needs is designed for J's
Core, shared with J's Industrial, and still to be built.

## To build

### Belts and small bodies

Asteroid belts are fields of rocks floating in their system's space ([The universe](universe.md)). **Asteroids come in
real types**, and the type says what they give; a spectrum shows the type before anyone goes, as real asteroids are
classed by their colour:

| Type | What it is | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C**, carbonaceous | the commonest | **water** and organics |
| **S**, stony | | silicates, and some iron and nickel |
| **M**, metallic | like Psyche | **iron, nickel and the platinum metals** |

- **Mining** is done **by flying**, with a starship's mining laser, as in No Man's Sky; by **mining probes**, which go
  to a chosen asteroid, mine on their own and send their cargo back; and the small bodies that aren't round (Phobos,
  Deimos, Psyche, Arrokoth, comet 67P) only by probe.
- **The water of C-type asteroids** becomes propellant in space itself: "water is the oil of space", as asteroid mining
  companies say; AstroForge launched the first commercial mission to an asteroid in 2023.

### The Deep Miner

On the ocean worlds (Tau Ceti f, 82 Eridani d, LHS 1140 b), the **Deep Miner**, a multiblock, **drills through the ice
and goes down through the ocean** to the layers of high-pressure ice. It brings up their **clathrates**, methane,
**xenon** and krypton, and **samples of ice VII** in a pressure cell, for science. It spends a great deal of energy;
with J's Computers installed, Operations command it.

### Giant planets

- A **shallow aeroprobe** gathers each giant's air: hydrogen, helium, helium-3 and deuterium from Jupiter and Saturn;
  methane and ammonia as well from Uranus, Neptune and Oceanus.
- A **deep aeroprobe** (T7) goes after the **metallic hydrogen** of Jupiter and Saturn.
- Every aeroprobe has **a hull limit**: sent too deep, it is crushed. Galileo's probe lasted 58 minutes in Jupiter.
- A starship's **gas scoop** gathers the upper layers, skimming past ([Starships](starships.md)).

### Places with no world

| Place | What is gathered | With what |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lich**, the pulsar | **neutronium** | a **skimming probe** that dives at the neutron star and comes back with the neutronium in containment |
| **Sirius B**, the white dwarf | **degenerate matter** | the same skimming probe |
| **The antiproton belts** | **antimatter** | **collector satellites** ([Satellites](satellites.md)) |
| **The kilonova remnant** | gold, platinum, uranium and **illaenium** | a starship's collector, or probes |
| **The Galactic Core** | **dark matter** | a **collector** on the ground of Hera, faster for the density at the centre |

**Containment.** Exotic matter is kept in **containment that spends energy**: without energy, it stops taking more and
**releases nothing**, so there are no accidents. The containment blocks are J's Core's, shared with J's Industrial, and
J's Space alone uses them for what it gathers at dead stars and radiation belts.
