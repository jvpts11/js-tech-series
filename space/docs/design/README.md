# J's Space: the design

This folder contains the design spec of J's Space. The entire mod is made based on these documents, with the sole
purpose of not only making every system that will exist sane and easy to build by anyone, but also of leaving no doubt
about the things yet to be made.

Each page covers one subject. Inside a page, what the mod already does comes first, and what is designed but not built
yet is in a section called **To build**. **Nothing of J's Space is built yet**, so every page is, for now, a design
waiting to be made, and the order below says what comes first. Every number in these pages is a first estimate, to be
tuned in play.

## The order of building

1. **J's Core's kits** J's Space stands on: moving structures, sealed rooms and the Atmosphere Detector, dimension rules
   by altitude, structure generation, milestones and the knowledge gate, the basic lines and battery, the containment of
   exotic matter. [The series](series.md)
2. **The skeleton of the universe**: a system's space, worlds as data, generators and modifiers, the sky, and the first
   two worlds, the Moon and Mars. [The universe](universe.md)
3. **The first flights**: rocket parts, the hangar, the transporter, the pad and the launch, orbit and landing; the
   propellant machines; J's Space's generators. [Rockets](rockets.md), [Travel](travel.md),
   [Propellants](propellants.md), [Energy](energy.md)
4. **Living out there and knowing where to go**: suits and pressurized rooms; satellites and the ground; observatories,
   probes, the Space Research Computer and discovery. [Life support](life-support.md), [Satellites](satellites.md),
   [Exploration](exploration.md)
5. **The rest of the Solar System**: its worlds, cargo routes, the Deep Miner and aeroprobes. [Worlds](worlds.md),
   [Cargo](cargo.md), [Mining in space](mining.md)
6. **The edge and the enigma**: the hidden planets, ruins, Enigmatic sites and their technology.
   [Structures](structures.md), [Enigmatic technology](enigmatic-technology.md)
7. **Starships**, small, medium and large, and the worlds of other stars, with their life and cities.
   [Starships](starships.md)
8. **The great works**: terraforming and megastructures. [Terraforming](terraforming.md),
   [Megastructures](megastructures.md)

With J's Computers installed, its integration is built alongside each step ([With J's Computers](computers.md));
manuals, advancements and sound grow with every step.
