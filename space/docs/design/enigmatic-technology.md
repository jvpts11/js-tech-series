# Enigmatic technology

What a team learns from the Enigmatic sites and the cities: the technology of an older civilization, beings of fields,
so what is learned from them is **the mastery of fields**. Every step is something new, never a percentage bonus. The
sites and cities themselves are in [Structures](structures.md).

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. The knowledge gate of J's Core, which holds these recipes until the team understands
enough, is designed with J's Space and J's Industrial and still to be built.

## To build

### Comprehension

**Enigmatic comprehension** belongs to the **team**, from 0 to 100%, and grows with the data of the Enigmatic sites and
the cities, processed by the Space Research Computer ([Exploration](exploration.md)): a little from a monolith, a lot
from a temple, far more from a cathedral, a fair amount from the artefacts of the cities, and two special sources, **the
site on Eurynome** and **the "Wow!" signal**.

The recipes are **always shown** in JEI and EMI, with a lock and the comprehension they ask for; a machine refuses to
run one until the team has it.

**Knowledge fragments** are an ingredient found **only at the sites**, inside the crystals of the temples and in the
cathedrals. They are the exception to the rule that everything has another source, and they exist to take players to the
sites; the sites never run out.

### Contact

With high comprehension, **the humanoids answer** the player's pulses: their guardians stop warning the team, and each
city **leads the team to a hidden chamber** that can't be found any other way. Contact is the reward of understanding.

### Milestones

The first Enigmatic site; the first temple; the first tower opened; 50% and 100% comprehension.
