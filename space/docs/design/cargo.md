# Cargo

How things move between worlds with nobody aboard: cargo routes, freighters, and the network that asks for them. How
rockets fly is in [Travel](travel.md), and how they are built in [Rockets](rockets.md).

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet.

## To build

### Routes

A **cargo route** is set up at Mission Control:

- the **origin pad** and the **destination pad**;
- the route's **reusable rockets**, one or several;
- the **manifest**: items and fluids, there and back;
- **when it leaves**: by hand, when the cargo is ready, or on a schedule;
- and a choice: **wait for the window**, spending less, or **go now**, spending more.

**A route needs contact.** Mission Control has to talk to both ends: communication satellites and relays within a system
([Satellites](satellites.md)). A route between stars needs the **instant link**
([Enigmatic technology](enigmatic-technology.md)) or a **chain of relays**, system by system. Without contact, the route
waits.

### The flight

The rocket **really crosses the system's space** on its autopilot, in sight ([Travel](travel.md)), with the cruise only
as a timer. It **lands on the destination pad**, as precisely as its guidance and the navigation satellites allow,
**unloads** into the pad's item port, which can lead to pipes, conveyors or a network, loads the way back and returns.
Its lower stage lands on its platform, and the hangar repairs it.

**What it carries:** the **cargo container** for items and **tanks** for fluids, including propellant made on one world
and taken to another.

### Freighters

A medium or large starship on **autopilot** runs routes too, between systems as well: logistics between the stars
([Starships](starships.md)).

### With J's Computers

With the instant link, **the networks of two worlds federate**, and the home network **sees the storage** of a base on
Mars. When **autocrafting** needs something that lies on another world, **the network asks for a cargo flight** on its
own. It is the series' principle: logistics are automatic, and the network runs them.

### Without rockets

The **Mass Driver** and the **Space Elevator** launch cargo more cheaply on fixed routes
([Megastructures](megastructures.md)). **Mining probes** send their cargo in **capsules**, to the home pad or to a
station ([Mining in space](mining.md)).
