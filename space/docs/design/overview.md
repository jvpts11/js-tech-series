# Overview

J's Space takes the player off the Earth: from a first rocket of gunpowder to a starship crossing to another galaxy,
across twenty-six star systems and more than seventy worlds to stand on. It is one of the main mods of the J's Tech
Series, and it plays on its own, needing only J's Core.

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet: this design is the whole of it, and it is what will be built.

## To build

### The ideas behind it

- **Real by the data, Minecraft by the look.** Distances, gravity, air, light, minerals and history are the real ones
  wherever they are known, and the worlds of the series' own follow the same physics. What the player sees is drawn the
  way Minecraft draws things: square stars, pixel worlds, no photographs.
- **Not a flight simulator.** There is real arithmetic behind a journey (delta-v, launch windows, gravity assists),
  shown simply. The goal is not Kerbal Space Program in Minecraft.
- **A journey is lived, not loaded.** A rocket climbs through the clouds in sight of everyone, the world turns below in
  orbit, the destination grows through the window, and a starship is flown.
- **Knowing comes before going.** A world is observed, flown past, orbited and landed on, in the real order of
  exploration, and a crew only lands once what kills there is known.
- **Failure comes from choices, never from luck**, and **protection gives the ability to stay**: a suit's only clock is
  its air.
- **Logistics are automatic**, and with J's Computers the network runs them; **combat is always flown by a player**.
- **Every world is its own**: its biomes, its landmarks, its sky, its minerals and, on some, its life.

### The shape of the game

1. A **sounding rocket**, then **orbit**: rockets built from multiblocks in a hangar, carried to the pad, launched in
   sight of everyone ([Rockets](rockets.md), [Travel](travel.md)).
2. **Satellites and the ground**: weather, mapping, communication, navigation ([Satellites](satellites.md)).
3. **The Moon**, then **Mars and the inner worlds**: suits, pressurized homes, propellant made where it lands
   ([Life support](life-support.md), [Propellants](propellants.md)).
4. **The outer worlds**: Jupiter's radiation, Titan's seas, Pluto's glaciers ([Worlds](worlds.md)).
5. **The edge**: the hidden planets, found by calculation, and the gate to the stars at Terminus and Oceanus
   ([The universe](universe.md), [Exploration](exploration.md)).
6. **Starships**: small ones to the nearest stars, medium ones across the Milky Way, large ones to Andromeda and
   Triangulum ([Starships](starships.md)).

Along the way: **the Enigmatic sites and the cities** of older civilizations ([Structures](structures.md),
[Enigmatic technology](enigmatic-technology.md)), **mining** beyond the surfaces ([Mining in space](mining.md)),
**cargo** between worlds ([Cargo](cargo.md)), **terraforming** ([Terraforming](terraforming.md)) and **megastructures**,
from the Space Elevator to the Dyson Sphere ([Megastructures](megastructures.md)).
