# Minerals

What every world holds: its rocks, its minerals, the materials only J's Space has, the ices of high pressure and the
exotic matter nature makes. The worlds themselves are in [The universe](universe.md); the machines that take these
things from orbit, from giants and from dead stars are in [Mining in space](mining.md).

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. Its minerals live in J's Core's shared catalogue of materials, which today holds five
metals; the registries of minerals, mineral families and host rocks every world needs are designed with J's Industrial,
in its Materials page, and are still to be built.

## To build

### How space minerals work

- **Every mineral has its real name.** Almost everything found on other worlds is a mineral that exists on the Earth, or
  in a meteorite: silicon carbide is **moissanite**, found in a meteorite by Henri Moissan in 1893; titanium carbide is
  **khamrabaevite**; tungsten carbide is **qusongite**, described in Tibet in 2007; ammonium chloride is
  **salammoniac**; the deep mantle's silicate is **bridgmanite**, the most common mineral of the Earth, named only
  in 2014.
- **A mineral the series invents is named the way real minerals are**: after the place it was found, as tranquillityite
  after the Sea of Tranquillity, or after a person, as moissanite after Moissan.
- **"Purer" belongs to the deposit, not to a new mineral.** The forsterite of Tau Ceti e is the same forsterite as
  anywhere, almost free of iron: what makes a world special is the grade and purity of its deposits.
- **Every world has host rocks of its own**: the Moon's regolith and anorthosite, the basalt of Mars, the sulfur of Io,
  the graphite of carbon worlds, the dunite of Vorna B, the glass of Specula, the metal of Ashval I. **On icy worlds the
  rock is ice**: on Europa, the bedrock is water ice, and on Pluto its mountains are. Ores take a variant in every rock
  they lie in, as on the Earth, and all of these live in J's Core's registries, so any mod can use them.
- **Space minerals are what J's Space's own recipes need**: going far brings back what takes the player further. For J's
  Industrial they are a **richer or alternative source, never a required one**: J's Industrial has a source of its own
  for everything it uses.
- **A creature's drop is never the only source** of a material: everything has a deposit somewhere.

**By probe:** Psyche (iron and nickel: kamacite, taenite and tetrataenite, with platinum metals), Phobos and Deimos
(rock like that of carbon-rich asteroids), comet 67P (ice, dust, **primordial oxygen**, glycine) and Arrokoth (methanol
ice, tholins). **By aeroprobe:** the giants give hydrogen, helium, helium-3, deuterium, methane and ammonia.

Three of these turn into play:

- **The Moon's oxygen.** Reducing ilmenite with hydrogen gives water and iron, and the water gives oxygen: the real plan
  for making air and propellant at lunar bases.
- **The perchlorates of Mars** poison whoever farms in Martian soil until it is washed, and they are the oxidiser of
  solid rocket fuel, as ammonium perchlorate is in real rockets.
- **Doris' vents**: an ocean hidden 500 AU from the Sun, with sulfide deposits like those mined from the Earth's sea
  floor.

### The other systems of the Milky Way

Nobody has measured a mineral outside the Solar System. The minerals are real, and what they do on each world follows
that world's physics: its star, its heat, its history.

| World | Rocks | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barnard b to e | bare, burnt basalt | iron oxides; the common rock of an old star poor in metals |
| Proxima b | reddish rock | **salammoniac**, crystallised by cycles of flares and freezing (ammonia, and from it hydrazine); gases of the stellar wind in the regolith |
| Proxima d | bare basalt | common rock |
| Tales | young crust; shores | **xenotime** concentrated at the hydrothermal vents of the shallow sea floor (heavy rare earths: thulium, ytterbium, lutetium) |
| Erebus | lava; basalt | **galena, bismuthinite, stibnite** at the surface of a young volcanic world (lead, bismuth, antimony) |
| Tau Ceti e | pale sand | **forsterite almost free of iron**: the star is poor in metals ([Fe/H] about −0.5) and the planet too (pure magnesium for refractories) |
| Tau Ceti f | ice over the sea | **ice VII** and **clathrates** in the depths, under the pressure of an ocean hundreds of kilometres deep |
| Gliese 667C c | sand and salt | **evaporites** of a sea that dried on the hot side: halite, gypsum |

| 82 Eridani b | a thin crust over **peridotite** | **chromite** in bulk, and in it the **platinum metals**, as in the chromite layers of the Bushveld in South Africa, the Earth's largest reserve of platinum; olivine, pyroxene |
| 82 Eridani d | the basalt of the islands | **ice VII** and **clathrates** in the depths; magnesium and potassium from a sea rich in chlorides |
| Pavonis I | the oldest crust | **zircon** in quantity; on the Earth, zircons are the oldest minerals there are (4.4 billion years, from Jack Hills) (zirconium for nuclear fuel cladding, and hafnium) |
| Draugr | iron and silicate | a sliver of the destroyed world |
| Poltergeist | **megabreccia** | slivers of **kamacite, taenite and tetrataenite** with platinum metals; blocks of mantle (olivine, peridotite); shock minerals in the seams (**coesite, stishovite**, impact glass) |
| Phobetor | **graphite** | **moissanite** and **khamrabaevite** in veins |

| Nox | ice | water and ammonia ices; organics |

| Archaeon | earthlike | like the Earth; limestone from reefs |
| Nereus | earthlike | **magnetite** in bulk, behind the magnetic field that drives the compass mad |
| LHS 1140 b | ice | **ice VII** and **clathrates** deep under the eye, on a world 10 to 20% water |

**By aeroprobe:** α Cen Ab, AEgir and ε Ind Ab give hydrogen, helium and deuterium. The brown dwarf Luhman 16 A has
**clouds of iron and silicate** (real: its twin, Luhman 16 B, gave the first map of the weather of a brown dwarf, in
2014), and an aeroprobe gathers drops of iron.

| Ithel I | **graphite** | **moissanite** and **khamrabaevite** in veins |
| Adamas | carbon | **diamond in the open**, polycrystalline like **carbonado**, the black diamond of Brazil and Central Africa whose origin is still argued and may be space (substrates and heat sinks for the finest lithography; armour) |
| Solum B | the shores of the SO₂ sea | **barite, celestine and anglesite** in quantity (barium, strontium, lead) |

**By aeroprobe:** **Ashval II**, a giant at about 2,500 K, has **iron vapour** that condenses into drops (real: it rains
iron on WASP-76 b, observed in 2020), the purest iron, with no slag. **Solum X** has **vanadium oxide** in its clouds,
as real hot giants do (vanadium for flow batteries and catalysts; on the Earth the same oxide is the mineral
**shcherbinaite**, from volcanic fumaroles). **Andromeda II** has superionic ice (below).

### The ices of high pressure

- **Ice VI** needs about 1 GPa and **ice VII** more than 2 GPa: the weight of an ocean hundreds of kilometres deep.
  Nature makes them: in 2018, ice VII was found trapped in diamonds from the Earth's mantle, the first natural ice VII.
- **Superionic ice**, confirmed in a laboratory in 2019, needs more than 100 GPa and thousands of degrees: its protons
  run free through a lattice of oxygen.
- **They only exist under pressure.** Brought to the surface they turn into common ice or water, so they are kept in a
  **pressure cell**, like the diamond anvils of laboratories, and carried as **samples for research**
  ([Exploration](exploration.md)).
- **In the deep ice of ocean worlds there are clathrates**: cages of ice with gas trapped inside, as the Earth's "fire
  ice" holds methane. Theirs hold **methane, xenon and krypton**, and xenon is the propellant of ion engines. The Deep
  Miner mines them, on Tau Ceti f, 82 Eridani d and LHS 1140 b ([Mining in space](mining.md)).

### Exotic matter from nature

J's Industrial makes exotic matter in machines; J's Space finds it where nature makes it. Each is the source of that
matter for J's Space alone, and a richer source for J's Industrial when both are installed.

| Matter | Where J's Space finds it | The physics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Metallic hydrogen** | the depths of **Jupiter and Saturn**, by a deep aeroprobe built to survive the descent | real: a layer of metallic hydrogen makes Jupiter's magnetic field. Mini-Neptunes and ice giants don't reach the pressure: inside Uranus and Neptune there is "ionic" water and ammonia, not metallic hydrogen |
| **Neutronium** | **Lich**, the pulsar: a pulsar is a neutron star | real inside neutron stars |
| **Degenerate matter** | **Sirius B**, the white dwarf | real inside white dwarfs |
| **Antimatter** | the **antiproton belt** of the Earth (found by the PAMELA satellite in 2011), and those of **Jupiter and Saturn**, predicted to be larger: a NASA study of 2007 worked out that Saturn's rings make far more | gathered by a collector satellite inside the belt |
| **Dark matter** | **the Galactic Core**: the density of dark matter is highest at the centre of the galaxy, so gathering it there is much faster; with J's Industrial, its detector on Hera works much faster | the standard prediction of how dark matter is spread |
| **Bose-Einstein condensate** | **the orbital laboratory**: in microgravity the condensate forms better and lasts longer, as NASA's Cold Atom Lab showed on the International Space Station in 2018 | real |
| **Helium-3** | the Moon's regolith; the giants, by aeroprobe | real |
| **Gold, platinum, uranium and illaenium** | **the kilonova remnant** (below) | real: the merger of two neutron stars seen in 2017 (GW170817) made gold worth several Earths |

**The kilonova remnant** is a place of the series' own in the Milky Way, with no world: the cloud of debris of two
neutron stars that merged a few thousand years ago, where the rapid capture of neutrons made the heaviest elements. It
is a place only to gather from, as a giant planet is, and the source of the heaviest metals at the end of the game for
J's Space alone. The real merger of 2017 happened in a galaxy 140 million light-years away, too far even for the game.

**Negative-mass exotic matter**, which holds a warp bubble open, has no source in nature, nor in real physics: J's Space
makes it in its own **Casimir generator**, and with J's Industrial installed, the Casimir Array is the scale path
([Starships](starships.md)).
