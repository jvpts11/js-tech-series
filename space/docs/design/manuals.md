# Manuals

How a player learns J's Space in the game. The guides are a framework of J's Core: the series' manual, the Technical
Reference, has a chapter for every mod, and every mod has a manual of its own, in its own style, made from the same
entries written once.

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. J's Core's guide framework exists, with the Technical Reference and the manuals of J's
Computers (the Guide to Operations) and J's Industrial (Plant Drawings).

## To build

### The Mission Handbook

J's Space's manual is the **Mission Handbook**, in the style of NASA's real mission documents:

- **checklists** for launch, landing and spacewalks. Apollo's astronauts carried their checklists **on the cuff of their
  suits**, and the handbook looks like that little cuff book;
- **a sheet for every world**, as NASA's fact sheets: its conditions, its sky, its landmarks, what can be found there,
  and a pixel painting of the world seen from orbit;
- the **models** of rockets, starships and stations, turning, part by part, as J's Industrial's manual shows its
  machines;
- the hidden planets, Enigmatic sites and cities only appear once found: the handbook shows what the team knows.
  Recipes, as always, are all shown.

The same entries are J's Space's chapter of the **Technical Reference**. Holding the manual key over a block of J's
Space opens its entry.
