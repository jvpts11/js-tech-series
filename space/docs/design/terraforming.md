# Terraforming

How a world is made fit to live on, and why every kind of world takes a different road. Living on a world without
changing it, in pressurized rooms, is in [Life support](life-support.md).

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet. J's Core's dimension rules, which terraforming changes stage by stage, already exist;
their rules by altitude are still to be built.

## To build

### One process per kind of world

Every world says in its data which **terraforming process** it takes, if any, and [the API](api.md) lets an add-on
define others. There are three families; the other worlds, icy, locked to their star or oceans, are not terraformed by
the series, and people live there in pressurized rooms.

**One project per world.** A world belongs to everyone, so each world has a single project, which any team helps with,
its progress visible to all and a milestone for whoever contributed. With J's Computers installed, terraforming
Operations follow and feed the stages.

### The Core Igniter

The piece the first two families share is a **huge multiblock**, a tower reaching down to the mantle. It drives energy
into the heart of the planet: it **reheats the core**, **rebuilds the liquid outer core and the solid inner core**, and
**forces the convection** that warms the mantle again. The **dynamo** comes back, and with it the **magnetic field**
that will hold the new air. It asks for an absurd amount of energy, the kind only the end of the game has: the Monopole
Reactor, antimatter, a Dyson Sphere.

**While it works, the world shakes**: earthquakes, and **volcanoes waking up**. On Mars, those of Tharsis smoke again
(Elysium erupted only about 50,000 years ago, so it is not much of a stretch), and the gas they release helps the
warming.

### Family 1: cold worlds of thin air (Mars)

1. **The Core Igniter**: the dynamo returns, and its field protects what is about to be made. Mars lost its air to the
   solar wind once its dynamo died, as the MAVEN probe measured.
2. **Warming**: **orbital mirrors** that send more sunlight to the poles, and **factories of greenhouse gases**
   (perfluorocarbons), both real proposals; the CO₂ of the poles sublimes and the pressure rises.
3. **Bringing what is missing**: comets and icy asteroids, by cargo flights and the Mass Driver, bring **nitrogen** for
   the air and **water** for the seas. A NASA study of 2018 showed that Mars doesn't hold enough CO₂ of its own to be
   terraformed: what is missing has to come from outside.
4. **Life**: **cyanobacteria** and **lichens** seeded to make oxygen, as cyanobacteria gave the Earth its oxygen; then
   plants (with J's Agriculture installed, its own).
5. **Stabilizing**: **climate satellites** hold the weather while the world settles ([Satellites](satellites.md)).

At the end, **the northern plains and the Hellas basin become seas**, where Mars most probably had an ocean billions of
years ago, and the sky turns from butterscotch to blue.

### Family 2: runaway greenhouse worlds (Venus)

Venus is the opposite of Mars: too much air, too much heat, too much poison. And its crust is a **"stagnant lid"**, a
single piece with no plates, so carbon never goes back into the rock as it does on the Earth, whose climate is what it
is because its plates recycle carbon.

1. **Venus' Core Igniter**: it re-differentiates the core and lights the dynamo again, as on Mars, and it **cracks the
   lid**: **rifts** open from one side of the planet to the other, and **plate tectonics** begins. It is the most
   dramatic moment of the game, with fresh lava in the rifts.
2. **Shade**: a **sunshade at the L1 point**, between the planet and the Sun, cools it. The CO₂ **snows** down, as dry
   ice, and the pressure falls.
3. **Detoxifying**: **hydrogen** brought from the giants ([Mining in space](mining.md)) reacts with the CO₂ and becomes
   **water and carbon**, the Bosch reaction, as Paul Birch proposed in 1991; the **sulfuric acid** is neutralised by the
   fresh rock of the rifts; the air slowly becomes breathable.
4. **Water and life**: the first seas, and the seeds.
5. **Stabilizing.**

### Family 3: almost-Earths (Kepler-442b, TRAPPIST-1 e)

They already have the essentials, temperature, pressure and water; they lack **life and oxygen**. The process is
**short**, with no Core Igniter: seed, let the oxygen rise, stabilize. It is the reward for finding a good world.

### How a world changes

- **The world's rules change at each stage**, everywhere at once: temperature, pressure and air (J's Core's dimension
  rules) and the colour of the sky.
- **New chunks** are born the new way.
- **Chunks already generated change slowly** near where people are: water gathers in the basins and becomes lakes,
  regolith becomes soil, green spreads from where it was seeded.
- **Each stage takes days of the Overworld**, three to ten; the whole takes some real hours, not centuries.
