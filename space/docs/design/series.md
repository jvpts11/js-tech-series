# The series

J's Space is one of the main mods of the J's Tech Series, with J's Computers, J's Industrial, J's Transport and J's
Warfare. This page says what it takes from J's Core and what it does with each other mod when both are installed.

## What exists today

Nothing of J's Space exists yet.

## To build

### It depends only on J's Core

J's Space needs J's Core and no other mod of the series: it plays on its own, with its own simple ways of making what it
needs. With another mod installed, optional integrations wake up; with them, J's Industrial's chains become the scale
path, and a server rule, off by default, can make them the only path.

**What J's Space builds on in J's Core:**

- the shared catalogue of materials, minerals and fluids, and its registries of mineral families and host rocks;
- dimensions made while the game runs, and dimension rules, with **rules by altitude**;
- the physics kit for areas and dimensions with laws of their own;
- radiation, with its two channels and its weakening through blocks;
- the **sealed-room kit** and the **Atmosphere Detector**;
- the **kit of moving structures**, which rockets and starships fly on;
- the **structure generation kit**, for ruins, Enigmatic sites and cities;
- progression kept per team, with **milestones** and the **knowledge gate**;
- the cable block, with J's Space's own lines: the telemetry line and the Space Research Link Cable;
- the **basic lines and battery** any mod uses alone, and the FE converters;
- the **containment of exotic matter**, shared with J's Industrial;
- teams, the Space Persistor, the settings screen and the guide framework.

### With each mod

| Mod | What happens with both installed |
| --- | --- |
| **J's Computers** | Mission Control, the Ground Station and the Space Research Computer become computers; the Flight Control Computer and the Aerospace Design Computer; Enigmatic hardware; Operations, programs, networks federated across worlds and stars, and autocrafting that asks for cargo flights ([J's Computers](computers.md)) |
| **J's Industrial** | its chains are the scale path for metals, propellants and circuits; kerosene, nuclear thermal engines and high-test peroxide; plutonium-238 for RTGs and fission for bases; its research computer reads astronomical data, and its discoveries about space ask for it; J's Space gives it richer and alternative sources: helium-3, metallic hydrogen, neutronium, degenerate matter, antiprotons, illaenium, dark matter gathered faster at the Galactic Core; its Casimir Array is the scale path for negative-mass exotic matter; its **closed exo-suits**, the Advanced and the Transcendent, take a space module and go anywhere; xenon from its Air Separation Unit; speculum metal for telescope mirrors; an industrial deck aboard starships |
| **J's Warfare** | weapon modules for starships; defence satellites; J's Space gives it orbital data, warnings of launches and the observation satellites' camera. The Laser Satellite is J's Space's own |
| **J's Transport** | its vehicles share J's Core's kit of moving structures, and run on other worlds under their rules |
| **J's Agriculture** | its crops in sealed rooms and starship greenhouses, and in terraforming's life stage; Mars' perchlorates poison soil until it is washed |
| **J's Geology** | J's Space's impactor is planetary defence against its meteors |
| **J's Oceanics** | its vessels and platforms work on other worlds' seas where the air and the liquid allow |
| **J's Robotics** | what robots may do on other worlds is its rule; J's Space gives the worlds |
| **J's Civil Works** | blueprints of bases for other worlds, and decorative aerospace blocks |
| **J's Overworld** | it is the Earth; J's Space never touches its world generation or its day, and only adds the planets to its sky, seen through a telescope |
