# Ship Fittings

## What it is

The blocks of a ship's kit that work aboard, and the modules' furniture. Once their part is formed, its model draws them with the furniture round them. They still stand where they are, and a click still works them.

## What it is for

- Flight Console: keeps the cabin's air reserve, two hours of one crew member's oxygen, and tops the cabin back up to the sea's air. In the dock it starts the ship; aboard, it opens the crew page ([the crew](jsspace:crew)).
- Pilot Seat: the helm ([flying a ship](jsspace:flying_a_ship)).
- Airlock Door: the way in and out.
- Airlock Cycler: on the airlock's wall. Docking Guide: in the Docking Module's ceiling, by the collar.
- Docking Collar: on top of the Docking Module, where another ship docks.
- Suit Locker: nine places for a crew's suits and bottles.
- Oxygen Panel: fills the tanks of the suit you wear from the cabin's air.
- Bunk Frame: the Quarters' bunk, a place to wake.
- Lab Panel, Sample Rack, Server Panel and Cargo Rack: the Laboratory's, Computing's and Small Cargo's furniture, drawn by their models.

The seat, the console, the bunk, the locker and the racks let the cabin's air round them.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Use the Flight Console with an empty hand: in the dock, to start the ship; aboard, for the crew page.
2. Use an Oxygen Bottle on the Flight Console to pour it into the reserve.
3. Use the Pilot Seat to sit at the helm.
4. Use the Airlock Door from outside to come in, and from inside to step out at its foot.
5. Docked, use the Docking Collar over your head to go through into the other ship.
6. Wearing a suit, use the Oxygen Panel to fill its tanks ([the lunar suit](jsspace:lunar_suit)).
7. Use the Bunk Frame to wake there next time.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] "Wear a suit to fill its tanks"
> The Oxygen Panel fills only a suit you wear.

> [!PROBLEM] "No oxygen in the cabin's air to fill from"
> The cabin's air is spent. Pour an Oxygen Bottle into the Flight Console.

> [!PROBLEM] "No air to breathe here: the bunk needs a sealed cabin"
> The cabin has lost its air. Mend the hull and let the reserve fill it.

> [!CAUTION]
> A Suit Locker broken drops what it holds.
