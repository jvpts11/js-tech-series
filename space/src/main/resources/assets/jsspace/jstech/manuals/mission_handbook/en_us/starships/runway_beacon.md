# Runway Beacon

## What it is

A yellow post with a green lens to the ship coming in and a white lamp on top, set at the start of a flat stretch. It faces the way you looked as you put it down, and its runway runs that way from it.

## What it is for

Landing a ship on wheels on a runway. LAND takes the runway when it is the nearest place to set down within 512 blocks and its stretch is clear. Any ship on wheels may use it.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

- [ ] Ground ... firm, level with the beacon's foot
- [ ] Length ... 64 blocks on from the beacon
- [ ] Width ... 13 blocks, the beacon on the middle line
- [ ] Headroom ... 6 blocks clear over it
- [ ] Traffic ... no other ship on it

1. Stand at the start of the stretch, facing along it, and put the beacon down.
2. At the helm of a ship on wheels within 512 blocks, press [[J]] for LAND ([flying a ship](jsspace:flying_a_ship)).
3. LAND flies to a gate a hundred metres short of the touchdown, then down a six-degree glide lined up on the runway's middle.
4. It flares low over the runway, touches down past the beacon and rolls on, throttle shut, till it stops. Then it lets go.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] "The runway past the beacon is not clear: no landing there"
> Something stands on the stretch, its ground is not firm and level, or another ship is on it. The stretch is looked at when LAND takes it, or as the ship comes near if its land was not loaded.

> [!PROBLEM] LAND goes to a pad instead.
> A pad you may use is nearer.

> [!PROBLEM] LAND never takes the runway.
> The ship stands on legs, or the beacon is more than 512 blocks away. A beacon aboard a moving structure is no runway.
