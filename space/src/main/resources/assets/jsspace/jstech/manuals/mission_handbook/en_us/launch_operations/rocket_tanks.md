# Rocket Tanks

## What it is

The blocks of a rocket's Tank, and of the rings that end a stage and meet the tower. A Tank is three or five blocks across, built a layer at a time as tall as you design it. Tank Walls ring each layer; the Cable Raceway runs up its side, the Fill Port sits low, the Vent Valve high, and Tank Domes close its lowest and highest layers. Standing on an engine, a tank two layers tall or more has Tank Skirt as its lowest layer. Separation Walls ring a Separation Ring; the Access Door and the Umbilical Plate are panels of the Instrument Ring. The Propellant Tank belongs to the lunar lander's ascent stage.

## What it is for

Holding what a stage burns: liquid oxygen and liquid methane, to its engine's mix. Once a stage is spent it comes loose with its Separation Ring, whose retro rockets push it away. The [mobile launcher](jsspace:launcher) lays a swing arm to each tank and each Umbilical Plate.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. On the Rocket Design screen, drag a Tank to a node, then drag it taller with Offset.
2. Stand each stage's tanks over its engine, and end every stage but the last with a Separation Ring.
3. Fill the tanks at the [pad](jsspace:launch_pad). The rocket's flight computer keeps what they hold until it flies.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The screen says a stage has no engine.
> It holds propellant that no engine burns. Put an engine under its tanks.

> [!PROBLEM] A tank loses propellant in flight.
> What it holds boils off as time goes by, the oxygen faster than the methane. A tank that has lost blocks is holed, and leaks the faster the more holes it has.

> [!CAUTION]
> A rocket's formed part holds when one of its blocks is broken, but it is holed there.
