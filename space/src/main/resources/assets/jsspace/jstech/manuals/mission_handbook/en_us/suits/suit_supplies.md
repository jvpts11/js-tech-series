# Suit Supplies

## What it is

What keeps a suit going between airlocks: the Oxygen Bottle, which holds half an hour of breathing for a suit's tanks, and the Suit Patch, which mends a hole.

## What it is for

Staying out longer than one tankful, and stopping a leak before the air runs out.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab. A bottle from there is empty: fill it before you go.

## How to use it

- Fill the bottle: use it in a sealed room to take oxygen from the room's air, or use it on a gas tank or line that holds oxygen. Its bar and its tooltip show the air in it.
- Pour it: use it while you wear the suit, or right-click it onto a Lunar Suit in your inventory. It pours what the tanks have room for.
- Patch: use the patch while you wear the suit, or right-click it onto the suit in your inventory. Each patch mends one hole and is spent. Used on a rocket's hull where a micrometeoroid holed it, it patches the hull too.
- In a starship, the Oxygen Panel fills the suit's tanks from the cabin's air.

## What can go wrong

> [!WARNING]
> Filling a bottle takes the oxygen out of the room's air. Leave enough to breathe.

> [!PROBLEM] The bottle pours instead of filling.
> Used while you wear a suit with room in its tanks, a bottle with air in it pours first. Fill it with the suit off, or once the tanks are full.

> [!PROBLEM] The bottle will not fill.
> There is no sealed room round you, or its air holds no oxygen.

> [!PROBLEM] The patch does nothing.
> The suit has no hole. On a hull, it mends only a micrometeoroid's hole.
