# Mission Handbook

This handbook holds what a crew needs on a mission to the Moon and Mars: how a rocket is launched, how it flies, how a suit keeps you alive, and what each world is like.

Each section has its tab at the edge, lettered with its code, and its pages are numbered by section: LO 2-3 is the third page of section 2. Hold the manual key over an item to open its page.
