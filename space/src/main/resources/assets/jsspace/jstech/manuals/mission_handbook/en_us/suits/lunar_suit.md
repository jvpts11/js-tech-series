# Lunar Suit

## What it is

A pressure suit in four pieces: the Lunar Helmet with its smoked visor, the Lunar Suit with the life-support pack and its tanks on its back, the Lunar Trousers and the Lunar Boots. It seals only with all four worn.

## What it is for

Living where the air cannot be breathed: in space, on [the Moon](jsspace:the_moon) and on [Mars](jsspace:mars). Sealed, it gives you the pure oxygen of its tanks, half an hour of it when full, and keeps you safe from cold down to -160 °C and heat up to 125 °C.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Put on all four pieces. The suit's controls show on the screen while you wear the Lunar Suit; press [[O]] to look at them up close.
2. Check that they read SUIT SEALED, and the time of air left.
3. Go out. Each second you breathe from the tanks spends a second of their air. Where the air round you can be breathed, you breathe that, and the tanks are spared.
4. Refill the tanks and mend holes with the [suit supplies](jsspace:suit_supplies), and fit [modules](jsspace:suit_modules) for longer and safer walks.

The air left is kept on the Lunar Suit itself, the piece with the tanks.

## What can go wrong

> [!WARNING]
> With its tanks empty the suit gives no more air, and you breathe what is round you.

> [!PROBLEM] The controls read SUIT OPEN.
> A piece is off. The suit seals only with helmet, suit, trousers and boots worn together.

> [!PROBLEM] O2 LOW, and the screen's edges turn amber.
> A tenth of the air or less is left. Refill the tanks or go back in.

> [!PROBLEM] LEAK, PATCH IT, and the edges turn red.
> The suit is holed. Each hole leaks four more seconds of air every second and lowers the pressure; with two holes it no longer holds oxygen enough to breathe. Patch it at once.

> [!PROBLEM] You still take the sky's radiation.
> The suit alone does not stop it. Fit a Radiation Vest.

> [!PROBLEM] The air never goes down.
> You are breathing the air round you, or playing in creative mode, where the tanks are not spent.
