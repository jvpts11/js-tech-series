# Ship Engines

## What it is

The blocks of a ship's engines and its fusion reactor.

- Main engines: the Twin Bells or the four-bell Cluster at the tail, or a Magnetic Nozzle on each nacelle. They are built of the Ship Engine Mount, Propellant Feed, Fuel Tank, Valve Pack, Thrust Chamber and Bell Segment.
- Air engines: the Side Intakes along the sides, or a Nacelle Intake in each nacelle. They are built of the Intake Lip, Intake Fan, Compressor Stage, Swivel Duct and Nozzle Vane.
- Thrusters: Blisters or Quads at the nose and the tail, of Thruster Blocks and Valve Packs.
- The reactor, a section of the spine: a Fusion Core within its Reactor Shield, with Magnetic Coils, Coolant Pumps and Radiator Panels, flush with the hull or as fins.

## What it is for

The air engines push in the air, and turn their nozzles down to lift the ship straight up and hover. The main engines push as the air thins, in the climb and in space. DOCK drifts the ship on its thrusters. The reactor powers the ship's parts, and its spare power fills the boost. It burns deuterium as the ship flies: a full load lasts four hours aloft.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. In the Starship Design, set a reactor in the spine and a main engine at its end, or on the nacelles of Stub Wings. The flight check asks for both.
2. Add air engines to take off straight up. With a VTOL TWR, their lift against the ship's weight, above one, they lift it off the land.
3. Keep what the parts take within what the reactor makes: the Starship Design shows it as Power.
4. Build the parts of their kit in a [shipyard](jsspace:shipyard).
5. In flight, read PWR and D2 at the top of the HUD ([flying a ship](jsspace:flying_a_ship)).

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The ship will not lift off straight up.
> It has no air engines, or it is too heavy for them. Where the air is thin they lift less.

> [!PROBLEM] The boost fills no more.
> The deuterium is spent, D2 at 0%, or the parts take all the reactor's power. A dock fills the reactor again as it takes the ship back.

> [!CAUTION]
> No main engine goes behind a Ramp Hold at the tail: set a Magnetic Nozzle on Stub Wings instead.
