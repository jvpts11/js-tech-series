# Mobile Launcher

## What it is

The platform a rocket stands on from its hangar to its launch, built by the [hangar's](jsspace:hangar) crane under the rocket. Its Launcher Deck, three blocks thick, stands on six columns of Pad Pedestal three blocks tall, in two rows the crawler drives between. The opening through it, as wide as the rocket's foot, is lined with Exhaust Frame, with a Hold-Down Arm at each corner and three Tail Service Masts round it. Beside the rocket stands the red Umbilical Tower of Tower Frame, five blocks square and a block taller than the rocket, its crane on top. The Tower Elevator climbs its shaft; a Swing Arm reaches each tank and each Umbilical Plate, the Crew Access Arm the capsule's hatch.

## What it is for

Taking the rocket to the pad and holding it there until it goes: the hold-down arms keep its foot down until its engines reach full power, and the crew climb the elevator to the access arm.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Give the bay a Bay Door and room for the rocket on a launcher under the hoist; the design screen then reads On a mobile launcher.
2. The deck points at the door, the tower at its far end: turn the capsule so its hatch faces the tower.
3. Confirm and build. Each part takes its own shape once whole: the deck, the tower, then each arm.
4. Build the [crawler](jsspace:crawler) under the deck, between the pedestals.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The crew access arm meets the capsule's wall.
> It runs from the tower to the rocket's side facing it, at the hatch's height. Turn the capsule until its hatch faces the tower.

> [!PROBLEM] A tank has no swing arm.
> An arm is laid only where it has room, and none at the crew access arm's height or a block either side of it.

> [!CAUTION]
> A launcher's part comes apart into its kit when one of its blocks is broken.
