# Capsule

## What it is

The crew's part of a rocket, three or five blocks across, seating one or three. Its crew lie on Crew Seats over the Heat Shield, inside Capsule Hull and Capsule Windows, the Capsule Hatch on the right and the [Flight Computer](jsspace:flight_computer) at the front right; Thruster Quads and Parachute Bays stand round the Docking Port on top. The lunar lander's cabin is Cabin Walls and Cabin Windows, its Crew Seat a chair, a Rendezvous Radar on its roof. The Instrument Ring is Instrument Walls, Avionics Panels, Battery Panels and a Telemetry Antenna round the Guidance Platform.

## What it is for

Carrying a crew and bringing them home. The capsule's hull, windows, hatch, heat shield and docking port, and the cabin's walls and windows, hold the crew's air against a vacuum. A capsule that has lost no block is the rocket's heat shield through air, and its parachutes open over it there. While the Guidance Platform stands formed in a stage not let go, the rocket comes down nearer the spot marked, in an ellipse half as long and half as wide.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. On the Rocket Design screen, set the capsule at the top of the rocket; the screen counts its seats.
2. Turn it with Rotate until its hatch faces the [launcher's](jsspace:launcher) tower, where the crew access arm meets the rocket.
3. Have the hangar build it. Its Flight Computer goes with the rocket and flies it.
4. On the pad, use a Crew Seat to sit on it; the crew stay in their seats as the rocket lifts off. Sitting is the crew's own choice: the count does not wait for it.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The screen warns No heat shield for air.
> The rocket seats a crew but carries no capsule.

> [!WARNING]
> A capsule that has lost a block is no heat shield. Coming down through air without one, the rocket burns up, losing part of itself and its parachutes; if the server turns burning up off, it cannot begin to come down.
