# Propellant Depot

## What it is

A white welded sphere on legs that stores one propellant for the pad. Its sphere is built as a cube of Sphere Shell, its edge an odd number of blocks from three to nine, its outside whole. A column of Tank Legs stands under each bottom corner down to the ground, all four as tall; a Vent Stack stands on the middle of its top; and the Transfer Pump stands on the ground against a leg. Once the depot is whole, the pump draws it as a round sphere.

## What it is for

Holding liquid oxygen or liquid methane, as much as a ball as wide as the cube holds, for pipes to take to the [launch pad's](jsspace:launch_pad) service port. A depot of liquid oxygen also fills an Oxygen Bottle used on its pump with oxygen, boiled back to gas.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Build the cube of Sphere Shell, its four legs, the vent stack and the pump. A Structure Projector shows the approved depot, nine blocks wide on legs two tall, and a Multiblock Builder used on the projector builds it; a depot of another size is built by hand.
2. The pump looks for its depot every two seconds, and forms it once it is whole.
3. Pipe one liquid into the pump. A methalox rocket needs a depot of each.
4. Run a pipe from the pump to the Pad Service Port.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The depot does not form.
> Its edge is even, under three or over nine; a block of its outside is missing; its legs are not all as tall, or stand on air; the vent stack is not on the middle of the top; or the pump does not stand against a leg.

> [!CAUTION]
> A depot that comes apart lets its liquid boil away: its pump holds nothing after.

> [!PROBLEM] The pump takes no liquid.
> It takes only liquid oxygen or liquid methane, one at a time, and only while its depot is formed.
