# Ship Hull

## What it is

The blocks a starship's hull is built of: Hull Frame and Hull Plating, Thermal Plating, Livery Plating and Carbon Edge outside; Deck Plate and Deck Grating underfoot; Conduit, Cable Tray, Light Panel and Overhead Panel above; Bulkheads and Pressure Doors between sections; Access Panels and Viewports in the walls; Canopy Frame and Canopy Glass over the cockpit; and the holds' Tie-Down Deck, Ramp Hinge and Ramp Plate.

They are part of the ship's kit, sixty-one blocks the same for every maker. Each shows one face on all six sides, so it is known from any side while the ship is built.

## What it is for

Building the spine's sections, each a ring of hull four blocks wide and three high. In a shipyard's plan, a part forms into its model as its last block goes in. Its blocks take the model's shapes, the floor and ceiling leaving room to walk inside, and the part is drawn in its maker's shape and the ship's finish. Every block holds the cabin's air against the vacuum, and the windows are glass you see through.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab. The Starship Design counts the blocks a ship needs.

## How to use it

1. Send a design to the dock of a [shipyard](jsspace:shipyard).
2. Place each block on its ghost in the hologram, layer by layer, or let the robot arms place them.
3. Watch each part form as its last block goes in.
4. To mend a hole, put the right block back where it was.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A block breaks and drops nothing.
> Kit blocks are cut with a pickaxe.

> [!PROBLEM] A formed part shows a hole.
> A block of it was broken. The part holds round the hole, in the dock and aboard, but does only what its blocks still in it can: a holed engine pushes less, a holed wing holds less up, a holed reactor makes less power. The right block put back mends it.

> [!PROBLEM] You may not place or break a block aboard.
> Only the ship's pilots change its blocks ([the crew](jsspace:crew)).

> [!CAUTION]
> Outside a shipyard's plan, a kit block is a plain block and forms nothing.
