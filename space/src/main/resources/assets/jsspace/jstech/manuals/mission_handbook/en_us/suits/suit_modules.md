# Suit Modules

## What it is

Parts fitted to a suit's pieces so you can stay longer where a world is against you. Two fit the Lunar Suit and two fit the Lunar Helmet.

Table: The modules

| Module | Fits | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra Oxygen | Lunar Suit | A second bottle for the pack: its tanks hold half as much air again. |
| Radiation Vest | Lunar Suit | A vest worn under the suit that stops half the sky's radiation. |
| Suit Lamps | Lunar Helmet | Two lamps on the helmet's sides that light the way ahead. |
| Gold Visor | Lunar Helmet | A gold-coated visor, lowered over the helmet's own, that cuts all the Sun's glare. |

## What it is for

Longer walks, a smaller dose, light in the dark and a clear view near the Sun. Where no air softens it, on the Moon and in space, looking near the Sun whites out your view. The glare does no harm; it only blinds.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Open your inventory.
2. Pick the module up and right-click it onto the piece it fits. It is fitted, and goes from your hand.
3. Wear the piece. A fitted Gold Visor shows lowered over the helmet, and the view through it is a little gold.

A piece keeps its modules wherever it goes. To take one off, right-click the piece in your inventory with nothing in your hand: the module fitted last comes back into your hand. Taking the Extra Oxygen off leaves the tanks no more air than they hold without it.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The module will not go on.
> It went onto the wrong piece, or that piece has one of its kind already. A left click only moves it: right-click it.

> [!PROBLEM] Extra Oxygen added no air.
> It makes the tanks bigger; a suit already used keeps the air it had. Fill the rest with an [Oxygen Bottle](jsspace:suit_supplies).

> [!PROBLEM] The lamps light nothing.
> They light only from a helmet worn on your head.
