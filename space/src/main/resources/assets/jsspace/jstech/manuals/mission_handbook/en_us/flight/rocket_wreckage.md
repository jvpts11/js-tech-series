# Rocket Wreckage

## What it is

The torn and burnt metal a spent stage leaves where it strikes the ground, as the first stages of real rockets lie in their fall zones.

## What it is for

Salvage. Its pieces hold about a third of the blocks the stage was made of, shared among them; the rest is lost in the fall.

## How to get it

A spent stage that falls leaves its pieces in its crater and round it, about one for each tonne of the stage. Under the sea they settle on the bottom. It is also in the creative menu, J's Space tab, but a piece from there holds nothing.

## How to use it

1. Before launch, choose where each stage falls on the STG page of the [flight computer](jsspace:flight_computer): ZONE for a fall zone reaching 250 blocks round the pad, RAD + to step it through 64, 128, 250 and 500 blocks, or ANY.
2. Wait for the stage to come down.
3. Find its pieces and break them: each spills what it holds. The wreckage itself drops nothing.

A stage that comes back to a [landing platform](jsspace:landing_platform) leaves no wreckage.

## What can go wrong

> [!WARNING]
> A falling stage strikes as a blast: the energy of its speed goes off at once, leaving a crater and throwing fragments of its aluminium shell. Keep clear of its fall zone. Where the server turns this damage off, the stage only lies there as wreckage.

> [!PROBLEM] You cannot find where the stage fell.
> Set to ANY, it falls in land already loaded round any player in its world, or where it came loose when there is none. Set a fall zone to know where it comes down.

> [!PROBLEM] A piece gave nothing.
> A stage of few kinds of block leaves more pieces than it has stacks to share, so some hold nothing. A piece from the creative menu holds nothing either.
