# This handbook

## What it is

The Mission Handbook: J's Space's own book, typed and bound in rings as a crew's flight documents are, with a tab at its edge for each section.

## What it is for

Looking up how to launch a rocket, fly it, live in a suit and find your way on the Moon and Mars, without leaving the game. Its pages are also in the Technical Reference, in the chapter J's Space.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Use it to open it at its cover; click to open it.
2. Click a tab at the edge to open its section; each tab is lettered with its section's code.
3. Read a page's number as its section's code, the section's place and the page's place in it: LO 2-3 is the third page of section 2, Launch Operations.
4. Follow a checklist from the top, a step to a line: the item on the left, what to do with it on the right.
5. Mind a caution, which keeps a part from harm, and a warning, which keeps you from harm.
6. Hold the manual key over an item of J's Space to open its page.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] A section has no tab.
> It has no page yet: its tab shows once the section holds one.
