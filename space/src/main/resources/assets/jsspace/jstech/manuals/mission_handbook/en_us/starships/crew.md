# The Crew

## What it is

Who may do what aboard a starship. The player who starts a ship from its dock owns it. Every other player has a role, each holding the ones under it:

- Passenger: comes aboard through the Airlock Door, sleeps in the bunks and passes the docking collar.
- Crew: works the fittings and the machines aboard: the Flight Console, the Suit Locker, the Oxygen Panel, the holds' barrels.
- Pilot: flies the ship from its helm, puts in and breaks its blocks, and has a dock take it back.

The owner is always a pilot, and alone sets the roles.

## What it is for

Keeping a ship to its team. A new ship's owner flies it, the owner's team is its crew, and everyone else stays outside till the owner names them. A player who joins or leaves the owner's team gains or loses the team's role at once.

## How to get it

Every ship started from a [shipyard](jsspace:shipyard) has a crew, and keeps it when a dock takes it back.

## How to use it

1. Aboard, use the Flight Console with an empty hand. Its crew page, drawn as the flight computer's display, shows the ship's name, its seats and bunks, then a row for the owner, the owner's team, everyone else and each player named, five rows a page. The crew and the pilots read it; only the owner changes it.
2. Turn the pages with the keys along the top.
3. As owner, choose a row by its left key or a click, then give it a role by a right key: PILOT, CREW, PASS or NONE.
4. To name a player, type the name and press ADD or [[Enter]]. They come in as a passenger; NONE takes them off.
5. Pass the Airlock Door by using it: from outside it takes you in, onto the airlock's floor; from inside it sets you outside, at its foot.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] You are told the ship is someone else's.
> Its owner has not let you aboard. Ask to be named.

> [!PROBLEM] "Only this ship's pilots may do that"
> The helm and the ship's blocks are a pilot's.

> [!PROBLEM] "No player of that name has played here"
> A player named must have played on this server.

> [!WARNING]
> Out of the airlock in space, you are in the vacuum. Wear a suit ([the lunar suit](jsspace:lunar_suit)).
