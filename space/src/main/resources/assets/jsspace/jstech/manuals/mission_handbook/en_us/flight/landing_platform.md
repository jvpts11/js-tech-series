# Landing Platform

## What it is

A square of Landing Pads with the Landing Platform, its terminal, in the middle. A spent stage comes back to it and lands on it, as a booster lands on its landing zone.

## What it is for

Getting a stage back whole instead of losing it as [wreckage](jsspace:rocket_wreckage).

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. Lay a square of Landing Pads with the Landing Platform in its middle: 9 by 9 blocks for stages up to 3 blocks wide, 15 by 15 blocks for stages up to 5 blocks wide. It forms once the square is whole.
2. Give the stage landing legs and an engine.
3. Before launch, on the STG page of the [flight computer](jsspace:flight_computer), choose the stage on the left and press PLAT. It picks the nearest platform in this world that you or your team may use and that is wide enough; PLAT + picks the next. The page shows its number, width, distance and whether it is FREE.
4. When the stage comes loose, it flies back high over the platform, comes straight down on its engine and touches down in the middle.

Built on a raft or a barge afloat, it is a floating platform: the stage finds it wherever it has gone. Its screen shows it waiting while it is free, and working while it holds a stage.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] PLAT does nothing.
> The stage has no landing legs or no engine, or no platform you may use here is wide enough: its side must be three times the stage's width.

> [!PROBLEM] The stage fell instead of landing.
> The platform held a stage already, was not formed, or stood in another world. A platform holds one stage at a time, and is free again a few seconds after that stage is taken away or moved off it.

> [!PROBLEM] STG shows the platform GONE.
> It has been undone or broken. Make it whole again, or set the stage to another one.
