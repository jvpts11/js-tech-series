# Wings and Landing Gear

## What it is

The blocks of a ship's wings, fins and landing gear.

- Wings, at the wings' root: the Blended Delta, the Stub Wings that carry the nacelles, or the Swept Wings. They are built of Wing Spar, Wing Skin, Flap Actuator and Wing Tip Light, a Carbon Edge in front.
- Fins, over the tail: the Twin Canted Fins or a Single Fin, of Control Surfaces.
- Gear, under the ship: Wheels or Legs, each Landing Strut ending in a Wheel or a Landing Foot, the front ones behind Gear Bay Doors.

Two more blocks of the kit go on the hull: the Nose Sensor at the cockpit's nose, and the Antenna Feed in the Computing module's ceiling.

## What it is for

Wings hold the ship up as it flies: more the faster it goes, as the square of its speed, and less in thinner air. Too slow for its wings and its air engines, it sinks. The gear sets it on the land. Wheels roll on as down a runway, and land on one ([the runway beacon](jsspace:runway_beacon)); feet stand where it comes down, on rough ground.

The gear is down on the land, while hovering and within ten blocks of the ground, and goes up once the ship flies clear. It folds flat into the floor in about two seconds, the struts first and the bay doors shutting after, and comes down the same way back. The wheels turn as it rolls.

## How to get it

From the creative menu, J's Space tab.

## How to use it

1. In the Starship Design, snap wings to the wings' root and fins to the tail.
2. Choose Wheels to roll on runways and pads, Legs for rough ground. The flight check asks for gear.
3. Read GEAR UP or GEAR DOWN at the bottom of the HUD, amber when down.
4. On wheels, close the throttle once down to roll to a stop.

## What can go wrong

> [!PROBLEM] The ship sinks in level flight.
> It flies too slowly for its wings and air engines to hold it. Open the throttle.

> [!PROBLEM] A ship on legs will not move on the land.
> Feet hold it where it stands. Hover off the land to move it.

> [!CAUTION]
> Only a ship on wheels lands on a runway.
